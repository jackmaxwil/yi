`yi models recommend [--role R]` suggests three models per role (high, medium, low) from OpenRouter's Artificial Analysis intelligence index and the catalog's prices, and prints the arithmetic behind each: `intelligence^exponent / blended $ per Mtok x popularity`. One table in `yi_ai::recommend::ROLES` sets each role's exponent, intelligence floor, context floor and token mix. The advisor squares intelligence, and the summarizer needs a 200k context. The primary and advisor blends come from the user's last 7 days of root-session tokens, so a 97.8% cache-hit history is priced mostly at the cache-read rate. Popularity from the previous day's OpenRouter top 50 is a multiplier capped at 1.03, so it cannot beat a clearly better ratio. Only OpenRouter models with an index, a credential and tool calling are scored, and the output counts the ones it dropped and why, after the CC BY 4.0 citation line and the snapshot's age. `yi models refresh` is the only fetch: three GETs with the user's OpenRouter key, at most once per 10 minutes, and a 429 names OpenRouter's limits and leaves the last snapshot in place. Nothing is applied to config. The bundled `models` skill tells the model when to suggest a change. The model browser that #1756 also asks for is not in this change.
