The plan tool's `set` takes the whole plan as rows and lands it, saying per row what it could not do (Closes #1074). A model sent each change as its own op and a single row the engine could not take refused the call: a cycle edge, a `done` whose check fails, a label past the cap, a duplicate. Now one `set` with `todos` rows (a label, a state, and the row's contract, edges or delegation) reconciles the plan; a `done` row's contract runs, a `blocked` row asks the user, and each row that could not move reads in the reply as `note: <row>: <why>`, the rest of the plan landing. A checklist `list` works as before.
