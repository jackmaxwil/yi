Both UIs stay. The plan's UISCOPE group asked whether the solo TUI and the console should both be kept, and found nine things that are scope, not defects: about a fifth of the workspace's test lines defend the two UIs; the CDP client serves one pane; the terminal, browser and editor panes are used by people only; the proof-recording tests that the TUI rules require; syntect and its grammar dump; nine title scenes; eight logo masks; three incremental-markdown mechanisms; and the pane engine and the in-process shell share one crate. The owner answered "Fix bugs, keep design", so each is recorded as a named surface and nothing is deleted. Any later cut names one surface and has its own issue; the decision line above says how to reverse this.

Three statements about the console were false, and this change corrects them. The first two are in docs/YI_DESIGN.md. Section 2 said the console does not depend on `yi-runtime`, and section 17.4 said it depends on `yi-types` and `yi-tui` only. Section 2's statement is true of the manifest and false of the build. Section 17.4's was false of both: its manifest also names `yi-browser`, and `yi-tui` depends on `yi-runtime`, so the build links it (15 files of crates/tui/src name `yi_runtime::` paths). Both sections now say so, as do the section 2 crate table row and the matching row in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, and section 2 names the seam that would cut the path: inside `yi-tui`, the pane engine the console reuses against the in-process shell that hosts an `AgentSession`. That cut is not made, and the dependency allowlist in scripts/guardrails/boundaries.toml is unchanged because it lists direct dependencies, which stay true.

The third is in the settings pane. The `tui.pace` row told a person the reveal pace was `solo tui only`, but crates/cli/src/shells.rs hands it to every console chat as well as the solo TUI, and the console reads it when it starts. The row's help now reads "the streaming speed in both UIs; 0 paints at once", and it applies at `next console`, the same word the title, browser and open rows already show. The `keys` row stays `Solo keymap` and `solo tui only`: it is the one setting console chats do not honour. A new test in crates/console/tests/chrome.rs draws the real rows in a Settings pane and reads the Reveal pace % row.

One finding is left as it is. The `unwrap_or_default()` on the syntect grammar dump in crates/tui/src/highlight.rs is already covered, because `the_cut_grammar_set_keeps_every_named_language` fails the build if the dump loads empty. The feature ledger is not touched: its console settings pane row already says a console chat reveals streamed text at the configured `tui.pace`, so only the pane's label contradicted it.
