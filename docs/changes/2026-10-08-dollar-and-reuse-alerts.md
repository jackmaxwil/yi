Three cost guards (Closes #1135, Refs #682). `spend.alertUsd` warns each time a session and its children cross another multiple of billed dollars, with the `yi stats` call that breaks the spend down; a child's spend now travels on its `ChildUpdate` as `cost`. A route that writes more to the prompt cache than it reads back over ten requests now raises a `cache_alert`, which the recorded Sol session would have triggered. `compaction.at` lets a root session compact before its context reaches the window. The per-day dollar alert needs spend summed across sessions and lands with the cross-session rollup in #1137.
