Yi Desktop's right-click menu on a pane or its tab now offers "Always open here" and "Never open by itself". Never stops that kind of pane from opening by itself; always opens it in that place in every session, with something to show or not. The choice in force is ticked, picking it again takes it back, and both are kept as one line a pane in the settings file (Layout, "Panes that open by themselves"). A right-click on a tab behind another pane now opens that pane's menu; before, it opened nothing.
