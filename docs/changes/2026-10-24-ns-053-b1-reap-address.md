This is Bet 1 item 4 of Yi's next shape, the reap half (B1-reap-address).

A plan child's ending used to reach its owner as the verdict line plus a `<reaped_child>` block holding up to 2,000 chars of the child's last answer. The block rode every later request, and reclaim never cuts it, so twelve finished children cost about 24 KB on each turn after them. The verdict line now names where the product lives and carries none of it. An accepted child's line reads `plan: accepted "<todo>" (agent://<plan>/<todo>); its product is plan://<plan>/artifacts/<hex>`, the artifact the engine stored as the todo's output. A failed child's line reads `plan: failed "<todo>": <cause>; its last product is history://<plan>/<todo>`, the address the engine records as the failed todo's `last`, and a child the contract refuses or whose merge conflicts ends its `plan: refused` or `plan: failed` line the same way. When the todo has a contract, the list of checks the engine passed starts on its own line, so no period is glued to the product address. A reap outside a finish (an owner's `done` or `fail`) reports `reaped <child> (<status>): its transcript is history://<child>`, or says the child kept no transcript. `SubagentHost::harvests` is now the set of children whose finish is settling, so their reap reports nothing beside the verdict.

The zero-spend kill test asked that every address a finish line prints open in `read`, `grep` and `rlm.fetch`. `read` and `rlm.fetch` opened all three forms. `grep` opened only `history://`: it handed `plan://` and `agent://` to the file grep, which refused them as paths that do not exist. The URL grep now serves any address `read` takes as one: `history://` is still searched by entry, an `agent://` address that has a reap pin is searched as the `history://` transcript it pins, its rows naming entries `read` opens, and any other address is fetched through the resolver and searched by line, its rows named `<address> line <n>:` so the leading token is the address `read` opens, with the same 40-hit page and 200-char line cuts the history grep names, each with its own notice.

YI_DESIGN.md section 13.3 and the plan row of the ARCHITECTURE feature ledger say the same.

The runtime crate grows by 33 lines against this PR's base, not the -8 the plan estimated: the address grep the kill test required is the difference. Tests grow by 297 lines, not the 70 estimated: two plan_readers tests over a root wired as `yi` wires one (its plan tool, its URL-routed `read` and `grep`, its kernel), whose wiring helper is about 45 of those lines, and one fetch_grep test for the address grep's two cuts at their limits, one past each alone, and one past both.
