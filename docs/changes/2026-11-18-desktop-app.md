The Mac app is imported as `desktop/`, its own cargo workspace beside Yi's. It is a client of `yi serve` and draws its panes with the Rune framework, taken from the owner's fork at one pinned revision; it shares `yi-types` and `yi-browser` with Yi by path and nothing else, and it is built and gated on a Mac by its own justfile, so Yi's CI, hooks and ratchets neither build nor count it. The public mirror publishes the app's mockup addresses as documentation addresses (two lines in `scripts/mirror/replace.txt`), the secret scan skips the app's recorded journey scripts (two paths in `.gitguardian.yaml`), the app icon and one recorded kernel transcript join the blob allowlist, the spelling check skips the app's build output and recorded wire frames, and `.ruler/098-desktop.md` states where the boundary is. The feature that leaves Yi as the app enters is model auto-review, cut in its own change (#2302).
