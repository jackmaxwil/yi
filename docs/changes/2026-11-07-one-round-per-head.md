The review bot reads a head once. Until now a draft needed a second clean round, so the review job called `just pr review` twice and the second call paid for a full second read of a head that had not moved: #1977 and #1974 ran two clean rounds on one sha minutes apart, #1830 three, #1888 three with a void between them that cost $0.51 and a $1.09 round that asked every lens although round 3 had lost only one. One clean round on the head now takes a draft out of draft and lets a PR merge, `just pr review` refuses a head a round read whole, clean or blocked, whether the trigger is a reopen, the job's second call, a sweep or `--again`, and only a new head earns a new round.
