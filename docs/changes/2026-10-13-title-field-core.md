A console now opens on a startup animation that covers the whole TUI: every cell of the frame, the session rail, the tab bar, the composer and the hint row included, drawn last so nothing shows through. The scene is drawn once per console start by weighted chance: the Kali fold eight times in ten, the echo rings two, seeded from the clock. The fold opens from a deep zoom over 1.6 s and the `Yi` strokes rise through it from 1.2 s to 1.9 s; from 2.2 s the big name shrinks onto the small orb mark's own cells and the cells around it give the screen back a few at a time, and at 3 s it is over. Kitty images draw above text, so while it plays the session avatars, the notebook image and the orb are not placed: there is one logo at a time, and on a real kitty terminal the first avatar and orb placements came at 3.03 s and 3.16 s. Any key, or the first message, ends it at once. With no small mark to land on (no kitty, or no session yet) the name shrinks nowhere and the screen simply comes back. Interactive consoles play it by default; a headless drive keeps it off unless the config asks, so its frames hold still. `console.titleIntro` turns it off and `console.titleWeights` (scene name to weight, `{"kali": 1}` pins one, a weight of 0 turns a scene off) sets the odds; an unknown scene name stops the console naming the key. Both are rows on the settings page, applied at the next console. The animation is a pure function of the screen's size, a scene and the seconds since the console started, `yi_tui::title::cover`, so a frozen clock replays any frame; the strokes are the orb's own `MARK_STROKES`, and `yi_orb::mark_at` says where they land in its image. The console asks for a frame once every 50 ms while it plays and once more when it ends. Tests pin the signed distance at five points computed by hand from the stroke constants, that no cell of a drawn screen survives while it holds, the solid stem at 80x24 and 200x50, an empty first instant, the landing cells computed from the orb's geometry, that the screen comes back gradually and entirely, that the orb stays unplaced until it is over, the draw's odds at 20,000 seeds and the frame throttle; each was seen red against a mutated version. A real-binary console journey drives a start under the config, sees the hint row covered, presses one key and sees the screen back. `tui_see` scenes can set config with `--config '<json>'` in their header, and `scripts/proof/tui_see/title.drive` plays a start with the fold pinned on a frozen clock.
