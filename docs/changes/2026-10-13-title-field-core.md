A console now opens on a startup animation that fills its whole pane and then ends. The scene is drawn once per console start by weighted chance: the Kali fold eight times in ten, the echo rings two, seeded from the clock. The fold opens from a deep zoom over 3.2 s and the `Yi` strokes rise through it from 2.4 s to 3.4 s; from 4.2 s the big name shrinks onto the small orb mark's own cells while the field fades, and at 5.4 s it is over. The small mark is not placed until then, so there is one logo at a time: on a real kitty terminal its first placement came 5.55 s after launch. The field draws behind the chat, never over a cell that holds something and never in the composer, its inside or the status row. With no small mark to land on (no kitty, or a pane without a session) the name fades with the field; the first message ends it at once. Interactive consoles play it by default; a headless drive keeps it off unless the config asks, so its frames hold still. `console.titleIntro` turns it off and `console.titleWeights` (scene name to weight, `{"kali": 1}` pins one, a weight of 0 turns a scene off) sets the odds; an unknown scene name stops the console naming the key. Both are rows on the settings page, applied at the next console. The animation is a pure function of the pane's size, a scene and the seconds since the console started, `yi_tui::title::paint_with`, so a frozen clock replays any frame; the strokes are the orb's own `MARK_STROKES`, and `yi_orb::mark_at` says where they land in its image. The console asks for a frame once every 50 ms while it plays and once more when it ends. Tests pin the signed distance at five points computed by hand from the stroke constants, the solid stem at 80x24 and 200x50, an empty first instant and a solid name once the fold has opened, the landing cells computed from the orb's geometry, that nothing is drawn once it is over, that the small mark is unplaced until then, the draw's odds at 20,000 seeds and the frame throttle; each was seen red against a mutated version. `tui_see` scenes can set config with `--config '<json>'` in their header, and `scripts/proof/tui_see/title.drive` plays a start with the fold pinned on a frozen clock.
