In Yi Desktop panes of one place now share a slot as tabs. Opening Code, Changes, the web pane or a report puts each in the work area beside the chat as a tab, in front; Files, Agents and Findings share a narrow column on the far side from the chat; the terminal, Logs and Trace share a short shelf along the bottom. Opening a fourth pane no longer cuts the window into a fourth tile. A press on a tab brings that pane in front, its cross closes it and the next tab takes the slot, and the tab keys walk every tab of a bar. Each session sees its own tabs of a shared slot and finds the one it had in front when the window turns back to it. This is the first part of stage 3 of `desktop/docs/layout.md`; the show action, preview tabs and the overflow menu follow under the same issue.
