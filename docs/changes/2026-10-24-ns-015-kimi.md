Yi reads the Kimi account's own model list (units K-kimi-catalog, K-kimi-wire, K-kimi-quota and K-kimi-login, stacked on ns-004's device-code login). `yi catalog refresh kimi` fetches `GET <api_base>/models`, where `api_base` comes from the `kimi` login profile, and writes `~/.yi/catalog/kimi.json`: each entry becomes a `kimi/<id>` model with its display name, its context window from `limit.context`, a completion cap from `limit.output` or the window, its input kinds, and its thinking ladder from `reasoning.type` and `reasoning.effort.valid` (an `only` model cannot turn thinking off, a `none` or absent object does not think). The `protocol` field picks the wire. An entry with no positive `limit.context` or an unknown protocol is kept in the file as its reason, which `yi doctor` names. Cost is zero, since the account is a subscription. Without a login the refresh row reads `kimi no credential (yi login kimi)` and the run stays green, as Cursor's does.
