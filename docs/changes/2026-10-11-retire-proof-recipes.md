`just tui-proof` and `just console-proof` are retired. A change a person has to look at gets its proof from `just tui-see run <scene>`: its snaps are the stills, and `--video` keeps the whole run as motion. That is `run.gif` from agg on the headless host, which the old recipes made, and `run.webp` from Ghostty on `--host term`. The worked example, `scripts/proof/demo.drive`, now carries a `#!` line and two snaps. `just console-pty` stays: it checks the console's kitty image placements under a real pty and makes no picture.
