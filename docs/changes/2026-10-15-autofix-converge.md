The autofixer can now land a fix that moves a measured baseline, and it keeps going while the review shrinks. Before, a prompt or schema edit made by a findings or gate fix moved the request budget, the tool-surface hashes or `schemas.lock`, which the fixer re-measured only after a conflict merge. The hook refused the stale baseline, and the repair turn backed the edit out while the summary still claimed it. #1412's two fixes each reported deleting a sentence from `identity.md`, and neither commit touched the file. The fixer now re-measures after every fix and commits the moved baselines as a `Ratchet:` commit of their own after the code commit. The stop after two fixes in a row now applies only when the last round held as many findings as the round before it, with a hard stop at four. #1386 went from seven held findings to three to two and was stopped anyway. The duplicate check no longer counts a PR closed without merging as a twin, so #1415 and #1416, which reopened the work of the closed #1411 and #1405, are no longer blocked at round 1.
