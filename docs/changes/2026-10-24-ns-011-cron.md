The schedule grammar and its calendar math (the `in`, `every` and `at` clauses, the five-field cron parser, the civil-date helpers and the ISO parse) move from schedule/mod.rs to schedule/cron.rs, unchanged, so mod.rs drops from 1,198 lines to 856 under the 1,200-line ceiling.

A cron whose day-of-month and day-of-week are both restricted (neither opens with `*`) now fires on a day matching either, as crontab(5) runs it: `30 4 1,15 * 5` fires on the 1st, the 15th and every Friday, where it fired only on a 1st or 15th that was also a Friday. A schedule text whose third byte falls inside a multibyte character is refused as an unsupported schedule instead of panicking. The local zone is read once and cached, keyed on the path `$TZ` names (or `/etc/localtime`) and that file's length, inode and modification time, where every cron claim re-read and re-parsed it; only a zone that parsed is cached, so a failed read is tried again on the next call instead of pinning UTC.

A session that holds no durable ledger, a child above all, now releases the shared heartbeat timer of its runtime directory when it ends, where the store and its timer task stayed interned for the life of the process. The timer stays while another session is bound to it, or wired to it and not yet bound, so a sibling leaving between a child's wiring and its attach cannot stop the timer that child is about to join. A durable root that binds its own ledger releases the one it was wired to, and a rebind releases the ledger it left.
