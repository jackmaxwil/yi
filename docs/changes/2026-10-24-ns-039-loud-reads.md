This is Bet 2's loud-caps part of Yi's next shape, one bundle of two units: CAPS-context-tools and OUT-read-grep-bytes.

CAPS-context-tools. `yi_tools::Cut` is public now, with named fields in place of six positional arguments, so a crate that holds a cap builds the same `[… kept of total what (CAP limit); the rest: next]` row the reducer does. A context source over its byte budget ends in that row, naming the budget, instead of `[... truncated: N bytes over budget ...]`. The compaction view says how many outstanding errors the 12-row cap dropped. A malformed edit header echoes at most 120 chars of the line and says how many it held. A default `read` window cut at 2,000 lines names `READ_LINE_CAP`, and `get_context` says its skeleton scan stopped at 2,000 source files.
