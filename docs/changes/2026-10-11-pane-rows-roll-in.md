A chat pane in `yi console` now scrolls a row at a time while it follows a turn. When several rows land at once (a paragraph break, a finished fence, a tool card), the pane keeps showing the rows it had and moves its bottom one row a frame toward the new end, a row more per frame for every 24 rows it is behind, and asks for the next frame until it catches up; a 200-row block lands in under 70 frames. Measured on a 90 tokens/s stream at 120×34: no frame moved more than one row, against 17 of 60 before. Scrolling up, a resize or a mode change shows the column as it stands, unpaced.
