Dead items in six leaf crates are deleted, each one found again at the fork before it was touched. The compaction window chain is gone: yi-context loses `Window`, the runtime's `Compactor` loses its window state and is built with `Compactor::default()`, and a compaction entry's `details` no longer gets a `window` key. Entries already on disk keep the key, and `CompactionDetails.window` stays in yi-types, so they decode and re-emit it byte for byte; a new fixture holds one the compactor wrote. `SourceBudgets.ledger`, which nothing read, goes too. In the loop, the `added_tool_names` filter (every constructor sets `None`) becomes a plain `None`, and lib.rs stops re-exporting `repair_tool_name`, `STREAM_RETRY_AT`, `STREAM_RETRY_TEXT` and `REPEAT_BREAK_TEXT`; the `ReasoningBudget` re-export the plan listed was already absent at the fork, so nothing changed for it. The `temperature` option of `AnthropicOptions` and `OpenAiOptions` goes with its three request-body sites and the `supportsTemperature` compat key on the three Opus catalog rows. D306 listed that key among the two compat keys kept; a catalog file written earlier that still names it loads and is ignored. `fib_dir`, `finalize_frame` and `radius_scale` become crate-private (`Orb::showing` stays: a test added after the fork seeds its orb with it). In ACP, `_yi/kernel_cancel` (no client sends it) now answers as an unknown method, together with `UserCells::cancel` and the cell's `call_id` that only it read. The `ToolExecutionUpdate` projection arm goes (no producer emits that event; it now projects to no update), and so does `daemon_protocol_version`. The `ToolCallContentChunk` variant of `AcpSessionUpdate` stays in yi-types: nothing builds it any more, but without it the enum's largest variant is 288 bytes against 72 for the next, which fails clippy's large-enum-variant rule, and boxing `ToolCallUpdate` instead would rewrite eight patterns in the console. In permission, `needs_host` goes (D312 names it; the runtime's `leaves_sandbox` has applied the same rule through `host_need`), and `ripwire_reads` is private. The method table in design section 17.2 drops `_yi/kernel_cancel`.
