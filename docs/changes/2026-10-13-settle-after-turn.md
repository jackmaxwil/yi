A turn cut at the reasoning budget, failed mid-stream or ended by an in-band error no longer waits up to 28 seconds for OpenRouter's generation record before it ends (Closes #1404). `OpenAiOptions::settle` carries a hook; when it is set, `run_request` sends the turn's terminal event first and then fetches the record on a thread of its own, on the same 3, 8, 16 and 28 second schedule, handing the measured usage to the hook. `attach_runtime` wires each session's provider with a hook that appends it through `spend::append_main_usage` as a main-lane usage record with cause `settled`, which the session's cost total counts. The assistant entry keeps its unknown usage, so the spend alarm still reports that turn's dollars as a lower bound; `yi ask` waits for the pending records at exit, at most the fetch budget (128 seconds), or half of what `--deadline` leaves, capped at its 10 second grace, so a one-shot run keeps the settled cost of a cut turn; a long-lived process that exits sooner loses only that turn's settled cost, which was an estimate before. The record's upstream rides the settled usage row's `details` under `provider`, the key `note_upstream` writes, since the entry was written without it. Streams opened without a hook settle inline as before.
