The term host now renders TUI snapshots in Noto Mono rather than JetBrains Mono. Noto Mono is the free monospace font closest to the owner's Monaco, measured by greyscale SSIM on a dense code page: 0.868 against JetBrains Mono's 0.843 (table on #1263). The CI image gained `fonts-noto-mono` in apex/the infra repository#634, and the local image (`just yi-term`) gains it here, so Ghostty finds the family it is asked for instead of falling back silently.
