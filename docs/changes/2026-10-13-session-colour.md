Each console pane wears its session's hue (Closes #1375). The focused pane's title is a filled chip in that hue and its border is drawn in it; an unfocused pane keeps the hue only as title text over a dim border, and a pane with no session (Settings, an editor, a diff) wears the muted grey. Framed panes keep one blank column between the border and their text. The command palette and context menus draw a muted border; the palette's selected row is a band in the lifted ground with bold text, and its prompt and cursor are amber. The selected settings tab and the active workspace tab are the same band, and the scroll thumb, copy flash, armed prefix hint, notebook results and settings values take hues of their own. Blue is left on links and the markdown H1 rule.
