E-dup-kitty gives solo and console one Kitty graphics support predicate and one base64 transmit loop. Console now honours KITTY_WINDOW_ID like solo, while tmux and screen keep text fallbacks and command-chord hints use a separate terminal-name predicate rather than graphics support. The shared loop preserves 4,096-byte chunks and the continuation flags for PNG and orb payloads.
