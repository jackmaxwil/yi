E-dup-kitty gives solo and console one Kitty graphics support predicate and one base64 transmit loop. Console now honours KITTY_WINDOW_ID like solo, while tmux and screen keep text fallbacks, read from TMUX and STY because kitty's own tmux advice sets TERM to `xterm-kitty`, and command-chord hints use a separate terminal-name predicate rather than graphics support. The shared loop preserves 4,096-byte chunks and the continuation flags for PNG and orb payloads.

Seen red: at `b362dd49`, `run_console` passed only `TERM` and `TERM_PROGRAM` to `crate::kitty::supported`. Its predicate returned false with `TERM=xterm-256color`, no `TERM_PROGRAM`, and `KITTY_WINDOW_ID=1`; the shared-predicate test now returns true for graphics and false for command-chord hints in that environment.
