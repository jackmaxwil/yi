A verification token, the `verification_requested` record and the `verification_stale` record now each have one constructor, called by the done path and by worktree acceptance alike. The done path decides staleness by comparing the token it built at the start with one it builds again at the end, so two builders that drifted apart would have refused a sound `done` as stale with nothing to say why. The contract-drift check and the refusal of a candidate or integration check are shared the same way.

Worktree acceptances verify side by side. The lane pool kept one verification lane whatever its size, and an acceptance waiting for it gave up after 30 s, so a second child whose check ran longer was bounced back to `candidate_submitted`, where `done` answered that a phase was missing and the child, already ended, could not submit again. A quarter of the pool now verifies, at least one lane and at most four (two in a pool of eight, four in the default pool), and a waiter waits until the session's deadline, or the verifier's own timeout when the session has none.

A worktree child's check runs once when the parent has not moved. `submit` checks the candidate, merges it onto the parent in a staging lane and checked the merged tree again, so a long check ran twice per child even when the merge changed nothing. When the merged tree is the candidate's own tree, which it is whenever the parent stayed where the candidate was cut, the integration now records the candidate's verdict under the integration's token, provided the verdict was reproducible (no checker read outside the snapshot) and the two tokens agree on the contract, the criteria and the output bytes; the `integration_prepared`, `verification_requested` and `integration_verified` records are written as before. A parent that moved, even by an unrelated commit, still has its merged tree checked.

A user's confirmation of a plan op the owner may not apply alone stands however late it comes. The answer was checked against a 300 s window only after it returned, so a correct yes given after five minutes was thrown away with "ask again". The window is gone; what keeps a late yes honest is the revision the question named, which the confirmed op already carries, so an answer to a plan that moved while it was asked is refused as a stale revision and nothing is applied.
