Yi Desktop can now give one pane the whole window: pinch out on it, tap it twice with two fingers, double-click the empty part of its bar, or press ⌘⇧↩. The same again brings every pane back exactly where it was. Tabs of the filled pane still switch in place; opening, closing, splitting or showing another pane ends the fill.
