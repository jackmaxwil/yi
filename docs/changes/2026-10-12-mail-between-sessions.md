A root session can now mail another root session on the same machine, in any repository (Closes #1245). `rlm.send("history://forge-root", "is the gate green?", kind="request")` resolves the address as a read of it would, by id or name and under the same wall, and appends the letter to that session's mail channel. The receipt says `stored`. Each root subscribes to its own mail channel when it starts or resumes: a letter becomes one todo, any number may be open at once, and it is delivered after the turn it lands in, shown as fenced data from another session, never as a prompt. A letter carries its sender's `history://<id>` as the reply address, the entry it went out from, the user's words the sender was serving, and a hop count. A turn woken by a letter of `n` hops sends with `n + 1`, and past `mail.hopLimit` (default 20) the send raises `yi.HopLimit` naming the chain, so two sessions that answer every letter with another stop. Only a root sends outside its session; a child is refused and asks its root. `rlm.request` refuses another session, whose reply comes back as a letter, and an envelope past the channel's 16 KiB message cap is refused whole. Fixed on the way: two channel deliveries in one second made two todos with one label, and the second was refused; it now carries the message's offset.
