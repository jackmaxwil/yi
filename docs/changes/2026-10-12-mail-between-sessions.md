A root session can now mail another root session on the same machine, in any repository (Closes #1245). `rlm.send("history://forge-root", "is the gate green?", kind="request")` resolves the address as a read of it would, by id or name and under the same wall, and appends the letter to that session's mail channel; the receipt says `stored`. Each root subscribes to its own mail channel when it starts or resumes, from the channel's start, so a letter sent while nobody held the session waits for it. Each letter becomes its own todo, any number may be open at once, and it is delivered after the turn it lands in, shown as fenced data from another session, never as a prompt. An `inform` makes its todo without starting a turn. A sender in the same process delivers at once; another process's letter waits up to a minute. `/heartbeat clear` leaves the mailbox alone, and `yi sessions rm` removes it. A letter carries its sender's `history://<id>` as the reply address, the entry it went out from, the words behind the sender's run, and a hop count: a run started by a letter of `n` hops sends with `n + 1`, a run the user or a clock started sends with 1, and past `mail.hopLimit` (default 20) the send raises `yi.HopLimit` naming the chain. Only a root sends outside its session, and only to another root; a child is refused and asks its root. `rlm.request` refuses another session, whose reply comes back as a letter; a `family://` or `kernel://` ref is refused, since the addressee would open its own; and an envelope past the channel's 16 KiB message cap is refused whole. Fixed on the way: two channel deliveries in one second made two todos with one label, and the second was refused; it now carries the message's offset.
