A cheap model can pick the lines of a cut output (Closes #1155). Naming `models.selector` sends a cut bash output's full text to that model, which answers line ranges only; the lines it names follow the reducer's cut verbatim, under a row naming the model. It is off unless named: on the bench both cheap models found the needed lines when they answered, but neither answered a 1,458-line build inside the 5 s deadline.
