The next-step hint engine keeps its eleven edges and loses what nothing reads (HD-D08-affordance-trim). `Edge.pitfalls` is gone, with its two size caps and the `pitfalls_size` check: no renderer ever printed a pitfall and graph.json carries none. The relation names `instead`, `before` and `after_refusal` are gone: graph.json uses `then` and `after_error` only, and the renderer never reads the relation. Five closed predicates that no host asserts and no edge uses are gone: `output_capped`, `todo_open`, `plan_ready_nonempty`, `worktree_unmerged` and `inbox_nonempty`. A graph or a refiner proposal that names any of them now fails to parse, in Rust and in evals/graph/refine.py, which mirrors the table. The shared fixture evals/fixtures/graph/structural.json drops the five conditions and the pitfalls and the removed relation names from its valid cases. Its two pitfall-cap cases become a parse refusal for an edge that carries pitfalls and one for a removed relation, and it gains a case for a removed predicate.

Not cut here: `done_refused`, `blocked_on(user)`, `blocked_on(child)`, `blocked_on(external)` and the `child_state` arguments `queued`, `failed`, `needs_you`, `stuck` and `repossession_pending` are also asserted at no seam and cited by no edge today. They stay because the haul finding behind this unit named the five above and no unit in this stack asserts them; trimming them is a follow-up that also moves the `valid` case in the shared fixture off `done_refused`.

The rendered hint text does not change by a byte: the golden strings in crates/runtime/tests/affordance.rs, which the old producers rendered before the graph existed, pass untouched. `Graph::check` stays as the shipped graph's validity gate, and the offline refiner (#686) keeps scoring edge patches against it.

The graph test that listed seven of the nine tool error kinds now takes the list from `ToolErrorKind` itself. The match that walks the kinds has no wildcard arm, so a tenth kind fails to compile that test file until it has an arm. It is walked, and held equal to the arguments `result_error` admits, only once the arm before it names it as the next kind: an arm that ends the chain early (`Timeout => None`) compiles and is not caught, because stable Rust cannot list an enum's variants and a list kept beside the enum can be forgotten the same way. The predicate table keeps its list of nine kinds: it is the closed vocabulary the Python mirror and the shared fixture are compared against, and the test is what keeps it equal to the enum.

D219's row in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md still names pitfalls and their 3 x 120 byte cap; this change drops both. The row is named here and not rewritten, because decision rows are numbered by the recorder (#975). The next-step paragraph of section 6 in docs/YI_DESIGN.md is corrected to match.
