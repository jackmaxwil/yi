The owner, 2026-10-28: "Reduce visual weight of the sidebar". The expanded sidebar gave every session two rows, the second naming what it is doing, its todo progress, its claims and its age, under a bold workspace header and two control rows drawn at the session text colour; four sessions took eight rows of a 24-row screen and one of them said `idle · 7s`.

A session's second row now belongs to a session with news: anything that is not idle, a red gate, a todo list, a claimed question, or the session the focused pane is showing (which is the row whose age a person is asking for). An idle session without news is one row, so the same four sessions take four rows. The `+ New session` and `⌕ Search` rows and every workspace header drop from the text colour to `muted`, and `PINNED` from bold dim to dim. Nothing leaves the sidebar: the state glyph, the label and the tile stay on the row, and the age, the working label and the todo count are one keystroke away on the row the pane shows.

Three drive tests moved with the layout: two clicked fixed cells whose row moved up, and `sidebar_rows_show_names_and_ages_newest_first` now reads the age off the focused row instead of every idle one. The proof frames this change was judged on ran `yi console --headless --size 100x24` over a `yi serve` daemon holding four sessions: eight session rows before, four after.
