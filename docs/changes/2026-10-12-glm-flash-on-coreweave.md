glm-5.3-flash now runs on one OpenRouter host, CoreWeave, wherever the repository runs it: the live evals, the review bot and the autofixer's first tier (Refs #1310). Unpinned, OpenRouter spread it over hosts measured at 51 to 235 tokens a second, and review rounds took 36 to 113 minutes. CoreWeave measured 126 to 235 tokens a second and answered a 40k-token prompt within 3 seconds. The live evals' old pin, DeepInfra, was answering 429 to every request. The review job pins strictly. The autofixer only puts CoreWeave first, since its higher tiers' models are not hosted there.
