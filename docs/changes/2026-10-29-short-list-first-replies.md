Yi's replies are asked for as list-first terse prose: the identity prompt's Voice section now opens with short sentences, the answer first, and one numbered or bulleted line per point, with prose around a list capped at two short paragraphs, detail only when it is asked for, no restating of the question and no closing summary. The sentence naming a banned tell is the whole ban list now: the wiki tells (#1862) that were spread over four paragraphs sit in one `## Machine writing, all banned` section, which is 1,398 bytes shorter than the section it replaces.

`evals/prose.py` scores a saved answer set and gates the prompt. It reports mean sentence length, the share of multi-point answers that use a list, mean paragraphs, hits per tell and rule mentions, so a before/after pair is numbers rather than an impression, and `--gate` fails when the shipped prompt stops stating one of the eight rules or spells a tell outside its ban list.
