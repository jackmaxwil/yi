Two instruction files that differ only in whole-line HTML comments now attach once even when a
comment leaves a blank line behind it. Ruler writes `AGENTS.md` with a `<!-- Generated by Ruler
-->` header above the first `<!-- Source: … -->` marker and its twin for another target without
that header, so dropping the comment lines left one render one blank line longer than the other
and the two hashes split: both files attached, and on a rules file past the budget both were cut.
The text the hash compares now drops the comment lines, drops the second of the two blank lines a
comment inside the body leaves, and trims the leading and trailing blank runs, which a comment at
either end leaves and markdown never renders. The hash is compared only
among the files of one session's attach, so nothing durable moves. Two files whose bodies differ
by a word still attach separately.
