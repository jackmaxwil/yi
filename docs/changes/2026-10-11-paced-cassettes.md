A `--faux` cassette can carry real timing, so a motion scene shows the cadence a person sees (Closes #1265). A cassette line may now be a line of a recorded session file: its assistant messages replay, each over the time its entry landed after the message that prompted it (the user's, or the last tool result), split evenly over the reply's deltas. A bare message per line still replays instantly, so no existing cassette changes. `--faux-pace X` scales the recorded time (`0` is instant, `0.5` is half). On a live run the delay is wall time; under a drive script with `tick` the provider keeps virtual time that `tick` advances in step with the app clock, and the drive waits until every update the provider owes is on screen before the next step, so two runs of a frozen scene draw the same cells. A frozen paced stream is not held by `hold`. `scripts/proof/tui_see/paced.drive` replays a reply cut from a scrubbed real session (7.7 s on the wire) as a four-frame strip.
