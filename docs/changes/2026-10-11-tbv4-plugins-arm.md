The tbv4-weekly workflow takes a `plugins` input when dispatched by hand: `all` (the default, and what the Monday schedule runs), `none`, or a comma list of plugin names, passed to the harbor adapter as `EVAL_PLUGINS`. A dispatch with `plugins=none` is the other arm of the comparison between Yi with and without ripwire on the same six tasks; the posted row's note says `plugins=all` or `plugins=none`, and that note is what tells the two arms' rows apart: the `+plugins{…}` fingerprint is recorded only in each trial's session metadata.
