The classifier now runs on TypeSafe's Jev through OpenRouter. `yi setup` saves `models.classifier: typesafe/jev-1.13` once an OpenRouter key exists and saves nothing without one; nothing is installed and `yi serve` starts no sidecar. A `models.classifier` that still names a Laya checkpoint (`english`) loads and means Jev at OpenRouter, ignoring the old loopback `classifier.url`, and `yi doctor` says so once beside the note that typed messages and judged commands leave the machine. Jev's `choice` answer carries `probabilities` and `confidence`; the skill pointer reads the chosen option's probability when `answer_confidence` is absent. The approval question is the V2 question the measurement scored, each value capped at 1000 chars with a `[cut: …]` notice in the question and a judged command past 4000 chars left to the person. Approval allows at 0.8 (`allowAt`) and 0.9 for a destructive command (`allowDestructiveAt`). An unreachable endpoint keeps the breaker: skill pointers fall silent for 60 s and approval falls to the reviewer or the person. `LAYA_API_KEY` and the minted `laya` key are gone.
