The review sweep's notice reports the heads still waiting, not every draft listed, and `SWEEP_HEADS` rises from 3 to 8, so the queue the owner reads is the one the sweep is working through. A head whose last round lost a lens or refuter answer is a sweep target, as a head no round has read already was, so the loss is retried once without a person running `just pr review --again`; a second silent round on the same head falls through to the fixer or the next push.
