The review sweep's notice reports the heads still waiting, not every draft listed, and `SWEEP_HEADS` rises from 3 to 8, so the queue the owner reads is the one the sweep is working through. A head whose newest attempt lost a lens or refuter answer is a sweep target, as a head no round has read already was, so the loss is retried without a person running `just pr review --again`: `attempts_of` lists every attempt on a head in the order it was posted, a round and a void comment alike, and the sweep re-reads a head for a loss only while the newest attempt is that loss and the head has `wanted` attempts at most, so a head whose lens fails for good is read three times and then falls through to the fixer or the next push. A lens or refuter call that fails on a rate limit or an overload waits `THROTTLE_DELAY` between its `THROTTLE_RETRIES` attempts, on the one throttle classifier the autofixer's `TRANSIENT` now extends with its disk and lock clauses, so a provider 429 is waited out inside the round instead of recorded as a lost answer. The sweep reads the shared `forge_pr.open_pulls()` listing #1899 landed; that paging is not this change's.
