B1-exp-d adds a paid, paired experiment for plan reader routing. Twenty-five questions have answers authored from Yi source at commit d08356d885a805108b6386842e1890ffa8fb15dd, with exact cited lines. Each question runs once under a root child and once under a reader with the same brief, on Kimi K3.1 followed by OpenRouter GLM 5.3 flash. The scorer accepts only the keyed answer with a quote retained by verify_quotes on the keyed line, records child token usage and reader prose refusals, and applies the pre-registered exact one-sided sign test and three-question deficit boundary. The trial log starts empty because paid runs do not run during the build; its rows are appended by the existing kill-test ledger.
