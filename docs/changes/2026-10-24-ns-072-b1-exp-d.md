B1-exp-d adds a paid, paired experiment for plan reader routing. Twenty-five questions have answers authored from Yi source at commit d08356d885a805108b6386842e1890ffa8fb15dd, with exact cited lines. Each question runs once under a root child and once under a reader with the same brief, on Kimi K3.1 followed by OpenRouter GLM 5.3 flash. The scorer accepts only the keyed answer with a quote retained by verify_quotes on the keyed line, and only when exactly one retained child session matches the requested arm, prompt, and payload with readable token usage. Missing or ambiguous evidence is unresolved, never zero-token correctness. Both families must complete before KEEP; the pre-registered sign test, three-question deficit, and any reader prose refusal can KILL. The trial log starts empty because paid runs do not run during the build; its rows are appended by the existing kill-test ledger with a $0.72 GLM reserve.
