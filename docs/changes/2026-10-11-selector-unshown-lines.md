The line selector now sees and adds only the lines a cut left out (Closes #1179). The cut result's lines are matched in order against the full output as the reducer shows its lines (ANSI stripped, the last `\r` frame, no trailing blanks, a repeat's `[×N]` count dropped), jumping past each row whose span is numbered in the file; the reducer owns that grammar (`yi_tools::reduce::row_span`, `frame`, `source_form`), so the selector is sent only the hidden lines, numbered as in the full output, and a line it names that the result already shows is dropped and counted in the row. A cut that hid nothing asks nothing. Dogfooding found 16 of 17 selected lines duplicating the kept tail of a verbose pytest run, and the reducer's own ERROR line sent again on a 3,000-line log.
