Reasoning streams again while it is live, and folds once it settles. Before, the default view either committed a whole thought into scrollback or, in normal mode, hid it, and a tool card always showed three result rows, so long reasoning flooded the screen and short results were cut for nothing.

Now a live thought shows its newest rows in a window of about a quarter of the screen under `thinking · 12s · 3.4k chars`, which never shrinks while the thought is live. When it settles it becomes one row, `thought · 24s · 8.3k chars`. A settled card keeps about a sixth of the screen's rows, twice that for a failed call, and shows the whole result when that is only one row more. Verbose mode keeps every thought whole. In the console a press on a folded thought opens it beside the chat, the way Inspect opens a file.
