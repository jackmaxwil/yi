Solo's composer and orb stay put while a reply streams, and a short terminal keeps a clean scrollback. When a paragraph commits, its rows are usually already at the top of the live region, cell for cell; they now become scrollback where they stand, so the commit writes nothing, history does not jump, and the region does not shrink and float the composer up. Rows that are not on screen still arrive through a scroll region, and on a screen too short for a region of two rows above the viewport they scroll the whole screen one at a time and the region repaints, instead of using a one-row region that a terminal ignores. Measured on a 90 tokens/s stream at 120x34: the composer never moved (it moved 27 times, up to 14 rows) and full repaints fell from 55 to 9; at 12x80 the scrollback holds every line once with no composer rows (5 corrupt rows and 7 lost lines before).
