Every cut inside a compaction view now says at the cut what it kept, what cap cut it and the call that reads the rest back. The brief section's demotion past `BRIEF_LINE_CAP` opens `[Earlier]` with one `[… N of M brief lines, the newest (BRIEF_LINE_CAP 120); the rest: id spans below, merged past EARLIER_CAP 24; read("history://self/since/0")]` row, and a demoted span carries the count of lines it stands for (`(#t0..#t4, 5 lines)`) instead of a bare id range. Past `EARLIER_CAP` the oldest spans merge into one line that keeps the sum of their counts rather than dropping the older ids without a word. The no-summary marker, which rides three times (the summary, the notice, and where the messages sat), used to say only how many messages it took; it now says `kept N of M (context_window K tokens)` and names `read("history://self/since/0")` as the call that restores them. A reclaimed tool result names the lever that cut it, `reclaim.keep_turns`, beside the request it cut at and the `read("history://self/<entry>")` that restores it, so the model can tell a cut of its own choosing from the runtime's. Nothing here changes what the ledger stores: a cut is a view over the append-only session.
