Every ⌘ chord the console decodes now reaches it in both Ghostty and kitty (Closes #1275). kitty's macOS defaults bind cmd+r, cmd+l, cmd+1..9, shift+cmd+d and shift+cmd+i, so the review and find chords from the previous change were dead there. Review moves to ⌘⇧R, find to ⌘⇧L and new tab to ⌘⇧O; the ⌘1..9 rail-slot and ⌘D / ⌘⇧D split decodes are gone, since both terminals take those keys; and the decoder matches shift exactly, so a shifted key no longer falls through to its unshifted chord. A test feeds every ⌘ key either terminal binds by default through the decoder and the keys overlay and fails on any that answers or is advertised, which replaces the runtime list of held chords. ⌘U and ⌘⇧U undo and redo a chat draft as they do in the editor, and the solo composer moves its ⌘ undo from ⌘Z to ⌘U. The keys overlay and docs/KEYS.md list the editor's ⌘S, ⌘⇧L, ⌘U and ⌘⇧U, and the overlay under ⌘ hints is a tui-see scene.
