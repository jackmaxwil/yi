Classifier approval is on by default, with three modes in `classifier.approval` (Closes #1037). `instant`, the default and what `approve: true` means, asks the classifier before the person and never denies an ask for want of an answer; `after-delay` asks the person first and lets a confident classifier answer once 30 s pass unanswered, keeping the ask open otherwise; `wait-for-user` never asks it, and is what `approve: false` already meant, `approve: true` with `askTimeoutSecs: 0` keeping its own old meaning (the classifier judged before the person, with no timeout handover). `yi serve` now mints the sidecar's key into the `yi login` store and gives it to the sidecar it starts, so there is nothing to export. The 120 s denial of an ask nobody answered is gone.
