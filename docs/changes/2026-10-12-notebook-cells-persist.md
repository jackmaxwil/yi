Notebook cells outlive the pane. The cells lived inside the notebook pane, so closing it (by hand or by the idle auto-close) and reopening showed "no kernel cells yet" while the session's kernel still held every variable, and a cell that ran while the pane was closed was never recorded. They now sit in `ConsoleState::notebooks` per session beside the review diffs; the pane renders from that store, and `apply_notebook` records every kernel tool call whether or not a pane is open. Because the store now lives for the console's lifetime, a cell is kept at most 8 KiB of stdout, 8 KiB of stderr and 2 KiB of result, and at most 4 images of at most 64 KiB of base64 each (about 48 KiB of PNG); an image over that size is no longer shown. Each cut leaves a `[…]` row in the pane naming what was kept, the cap and that the transcript holds the rest. Re-pointing a notebook pane to another session now redraws it from that session's cells.
