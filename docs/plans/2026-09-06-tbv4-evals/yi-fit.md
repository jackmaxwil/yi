## The dry tier still holds

`python3 evals/selftest.py` and `just postmerge-evals` are unchanged by v4:
they pin the run command, the usage parse, the all-or-none rule and the
three fixture tasks (`evals/README.md`, "Self-test", "Task runner"). Nothing
in the v4 contract alters what they prove; the two driver defects above sit
outside what they cover, which is why they survived.

## Summary

Four surfaces meet the contract outright (`--json --yolo`, exit codes, usage,
`--session-dir`). Three are one-to-two-line gaps in `evals/adapters`
(`SUPPORTS_RESUME`, the telemetry config line, the fingerprint's suite).
Two are defects in the driver that must be fixed before any paid run
(`--task-name`, and `runs/` versus `jobs/`, the second of which lets the cap
fail open). Two are moot for v4 (the eval permission profile, `answer.txt`).
One is new and on the path (ATIF, plan S4). One is red on this host and not code (the
musl target).
