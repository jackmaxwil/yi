# D406: a console message finds its ledger entry by what it says among the branch's...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A console message finds its ledger entry by what it says among the branch's entries
(the n-th identical cell is the n-th identical entry), from the replay and from a
new `_yi/committed{entry}` notice the worker sends as each user or assistant message
is saved; `_yi/fork{entryId, position}` makes a new session before or at that entry,
attached beside its source

## Why

The menu's ledger verbs (rewind, re-ask, fork, inspect) need an entry id, and a cell
carried none: live events name no entry and one entry draws as several cells

## Reversible via

Drop the `_yi/committed` emit and the fork handler, and the menu's ledger rows
