# D424: The dist binary's hard cap moves from 8 MiB to 9 MiB, still checked...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The dist binary's hard cap moves from 8 MiB to 9 MiB, still checked independently of
the measured ratchet

## Why

The binary is 8,372,272 bytes, 16,336 under 8 MiB, and the owner-approved console
workbench (a terminal pane, a browser pane, sidebar groups, a header row) is
estimated at about 1,450 lines, roughly 435 KB at the recent 300 bytes per src line,
while other lanes grew the binary about 300 KB in three days

## Reversible via

Reverse via scripts/guardrails/check_binary_size.py `HARD_CAP_BYTES` back to
8,388,608 once the ratchet sits under it again, plus every 9-MiB spelling in the
same edit: the docstring sentence, both error strings, the constant's trailing
comment, both `--selfcheck` assert strings, the `--selfcheck` pin `HARD_CAP_BYTES ==
9 * 1_048_576` and its comment, the push-lane mock pin in scripts/forge_pr.py, and
the cap sentence in .ruler/060-deps.md and YI_DESIGN.md §18.6
