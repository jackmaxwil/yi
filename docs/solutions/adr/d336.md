# D336: one status vocabulary for every surface

Status: accepted

## Decision

One status vocabulary for every surface: `?` needs you, `◐` working, `!` stuck, `●`
done and unseen, `○` idle or finished, `✕` failed, `·` unknown;
`yi_types::status_mark::StatusMark::glyph` is the only table of those glyphs, and
every surface that draws a state reads it: the console's `SessionStatus::glyph`
(sidebar, rail, workspaces block, palette, pane titles), the sidebar's child rows
(`sidebar::child_mark`), the TUI child card, the `/agents` roster, failed tool
cells, the plan tree, the console timeline and gate jobs (`JobState::glyph`, which
the status row and the environment tail's `landing:` line print); so `✗` is gone as
a failed mark, and `?` no longer means an unknown gate job or todo state, which now
read `·` (`$?` and the `⌥?` key keep their `?`); every mark is one terminal cell; a
child row hangs off its session on `├` (`└` for the last) at column 2, with its
glyph in the session glyph column, 7 on the rail and `cols + 8` in the full sidebar,
whose names drop control characters and are cut and padded in the cells the terminal
draws (`sidebar::pad_cells`, which measures the whole string, since `⚠️` is 1 + 0
cells a character at a time but 2 drawn); a session shows its three children most in
need first (needs you, failed, stuck, working, finished) and a `└ +N` row counts the
rest; a child's glyph takes the colour of a session with the same mark (a child that
needs you is red like a session that does), while the TUI card keeps D234's yellow
tone for a child that needs you or is stuck

## Why

`✕` meant needs you on a session and failed on a child, so a failed child read as
one waiting on the user; child rows had no connector and their glyph sat one column
left of the sessions', so they read as unnumbered sessions (the lane-5 dogfood of
2026-09-27); the three oldest children showed, so a fourth that needed you could
hide behind three finished ones; the owner chose "? = needs you, ✕ = failed
(Recommended)" (#764)

## Reversible via

Point `StatusMark::glyph`'s `NeedsYou` arm back at `✕` and its `Failed` arm at `✗`,
and `Unknown` sites back at `?`; the connectors, the column, the ordering and the
`+N` row stand without the glyph change
