# D336: sessions and children share one mark vocabulary

Status: accepted

## Decision

Sessions and children share one mark vocabulary: `?` needs you, `◐` working, `!`
stuck, `●` done and unseen, `○` idle or finished, `✕` failed, `·` unknown;
`SessionStatus::glyph` is the only source for a state a child shares with a session
(`sidebar::child_glyph` maps needs you, working and finished onto `Blocked`,
`Working` and `Idle` and takes their colour from `status_style`), and a child adds
only `!` and `✕`; every mark is one terminal cell; a child row hangs off its session
on `├` (`└` for the last) at column 2, with the child's glyph in the session glyph
column, 7 on the rail and `cols + 8` in the full sidebar, whose names are cut and
padded in display cells (`sidebar::pad_cells`); the TUI's child card and `/agents` roster
draw a failed child as `✕`, and the roster draws a running one `◐` and a finished
one `○`

## Why

`✕` meant needs you on a session and failed on a child, so a failed child read as
one waiting on the user; child rows had no connector and their glyph sat one column
left of the sessions', so they read as unnumbered sessions (the lane-5 dogfood of
2026-09-27); the owner chose "? = needs you, ✕ = failed (Recommended)" (#764)

## Reversible via

Set `SessionStatus::Blocked` back to `✕` and the child marks in `child_glyph`,
`cell.rs` and `agents.rs` back to theirs; the connectors and the column stand
without the glyph change
