# D300: a child's request is made of its brief

Status: accepted

## Decision

`rlm.run` takes `partition`, a list of Urls resolved at spawn by the parent's `Resolver` (walled as
its reads, `kernel://` refused) and inlined before the prompt as numbered lines in untrusted yard
fences, 64 KiB at most, for any role. `role="reader"` builds a question-child: `prompts/reader.md`
is its whole system prompt, its tools are a subset of `read` and `grep`, `turns` caps its requests
(3 by default, the last with tools off), its writes are walled, and it has no extension, kernel,
plan, schedule, checkpoint or environment block. `Standing::Reader` stands outside the worker cap
and sends no finish notice; `rlm.ask` runs one, reads its result and reaps it. A bare `rlm.run`
is still the full child until the default flips

## Why

A child was a full copy of the root: 19,764 bytes of skills, 21,265 of doctrine and ten tools
before its task, 19,835 input tokens for a 39-character brief (`rlm-27741/sub-709180e9`), the
context by address one fetch at a time, eight at once. The judge replay left Yi because `yi ask`
gave no JSON answer in 67 of 99 calls (row 0060). The owner chose "Only what's named", "Look it
up, few turns" and "A question-child" for a bare call (#809)

## Reversible via

Drop `role`, `partition`, `tools` and `turns` from the whitelist, `subagent/reader.rs`,
`Standing::Reader`, the turn cap and `rlm.ask`
