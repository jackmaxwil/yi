# D603: Yi Desktop lands as `desktop/`, its own Cargo workspace, after a staged...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Yi Desktop lands as `desktop/`, its own Cargo workspace, after a staged build on the
owner's pinned Rune fork, keeps its own gate, v1 uses today's server unchanged, and
the app owns its direct local integrations and Luau panes

## Why

The owner chose an in-tree native Rust app, a Rune fork that pulls upstream and may
take small patches, and a lean UI-first v1, Yi's dependency cap and review rules
make a separate staging repository necessary

## Reversible via

Do not import the staged tree
