# D307: a child's request is made of its brief

Status: accepted

## Decision

A child's request is made of its brief: `rlm.run` takes `partition` (Urls resolved
at spawn by the parent's `Resolver`, walled as its reads, `kernel://` refused,
inlined before the prompt as numbered lines in untrusted yard fences, 64 KiB at
most) for any role, and `role="reader"` builds a question-child whose whole system
prompt is `prompts/reader.md`, whose tools are a subset of `read` and `grep`, whose
`turns` cap its requests (3 by default, the last with tools off), whose writes are
walled, and which has no extension, kernel, plan, schedule, checkpoint or
environment block; `Standing::Reader` stands outside the worker cap, 64 held at
most, and sends no finish notice; `rlm.ask` runs one, reads its result and reaps it;
a bare `rlm.run` is still the full child until the default flips

## Why

A child was a full copy of the root: 19,764 bytes of skills, 21,265 of doctrine and
ten tools before its task, 19,835 input tokens for a 39-character brief
(`rlm-27741/sub-709180e9`), context by address one fetch at a time, eight at once;
the judge replay left Yi because `yi ask` gave no JSON answer in 67 of 99 calls (row
0060); the owner chose "Only what's named", "Look it up, few turns" and "A question-
child" for a bare call (#809)

## Reversible via

Drop `role`, `partition`, `tools` and `turns` from the whitelist,
`subagent/reader.rs`, `Standing::Reader`, the turn cap and `rlm.ask`
