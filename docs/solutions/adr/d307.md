# D307: a child's request is made of its brief

Status: accepted

## Decision

A child's request is made of its brief: `rlm.run` takes `partition` (Urls resolved
and walled at spawn, inlined as numbered lines in untrusted fences, 64 KiB at most
with a `[… kept …]` row) for any role, and `role="reader"` builds a question-child:
`prompts/reader.md` as its whole system prompt, a subset of `read` and `grep`,
`turns` (3) ending on a last word whose tool calls are refused, walled writes, the
user's gate rules, and no extension, kernel or environment block; readers stand
outside the worker cap (64 held) and report their finish; `rlm.ask` runs one and
reaps it

## Why

A 39-character brief cost 19,835 input tokens (`rlm-27741/sub-709180e9`), and `yi
ask` gave no JSON answer in 67 of 99 calls (row 0060); the owner chose "Only what's
named", "Look it up, few turns" and "A question-child" (#809)

## Reversible via

Drop `role`, `partition`, `tools` and `turns`, `subagent/reader.rs`,
`Standing::Reader`, the turn cap and `rlm.ask`
