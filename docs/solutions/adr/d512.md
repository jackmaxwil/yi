# D512: Subagents should be persistent in their two locations in the scrollback

Status: accepted

## Decision

Subagents should be persistent in their two locations in the scrollback: at start
and end

## Why

One shared formatter projects the durable spawn and end records into two concise
rows, so the live card no longer needs to become the permanent end record

## Reversible via

Remove the replay projection and restore the committed full Task cell; the added
optional wire fields remain additive and old readers ignore them
