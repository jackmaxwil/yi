# D454: a 429 whose body names exhausted quota or balance...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A 429 whose body names exhausted quota or balance (`exceeded_current_quota_error` or
`insufficient_quota` on any route, or billing wording on a Moonshot host only, never
a bare "quota") is final: no retry, and the error reads `HTTP 429 quota exhausted:
…`; `yi catalog usage kimi` prints the account's 5-hour, 7-day and monthly used
ratios as one JSON line

## Why

A quota refusal does not lift inside the 300 s retry wall, and a paid run must stop
on it and switch family, not wait (owner: "use my entire kimi subscription. then $30
openrouter cap"; kimi-code kosong/src/providers/kimi-errors.ts:1-22); Gemini's per-
minute RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED throttle also says "exceeded your current quota", so the
wording is read on a Moonshot host only

## Reversible via

Restore `is_retryable_status`'s unconditional 429 arm and drop the `usage` verb
