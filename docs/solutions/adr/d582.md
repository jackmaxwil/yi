# D582: A benchmark suite a gate or refine round reads is a row in...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A benchmark suite a gate or refine round reads is a row in evals/suites.json, a
Harbor row pinned by digest, and a gate refuses a suite with no recorded A/A
calibration

## Why

The weekly legs, the refine stages and the Terminal-Bench driver each carried their
own task lists and prices, and a verdict on a suite with no measured spread cannot
say what it could detect

## Reversible via

Read task lists from the drivers again and delete evals/suites.json
