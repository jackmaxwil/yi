# D437: a session's OpenRouter turn that is owed a settlement ends at once with its...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A session's OpenRouter turn that is owed a settlement ends at once with its usage
unknown, and the generation record is fetched afterwards on a detached thread whose
result is booked on the session as a `settled` usage record (amends D163, D175); the
hook rides the session's shared ProviderStream, so a side call's stream (title,
compaction) settles after the turn too, its own usage row booked as settled

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-12: "End now, cost later"; the record lands about ten seconds
after the drop and a cut turn waited up to 28 s for it

## Reversible via

Drop the hook from `ProviderStream`, which restores the inline wait
