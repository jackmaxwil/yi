# D484: A cell's stdout, stderr and repr keep their 65,536-char budget as the first...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A cell's stdout, stderr and repr keep their 65,536-char budget as the first and last
halves, and a repr past it spills to a file like a stream; the budget itself is
unchanged and still bounds host memory

## Why

Rev 3's View policy and D316's list of producers cut with no file: a repr past the
cap had no file, and a failure printed at the end of a long cell was in the half the
head-only cap dropped

## Reversible via

Restore append_capped's head-only branch and the repr's head-only cut
