# D195: a worktree todo is done only when its candidate and its integration both...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A worktree todo is done only when its candidate and its integration both pass and
the acceptance is recorded; every other exit records a disposition and never merges
to free a slot; a stuck child is reported once per episode: the running child's
`submit` commits its lane as the candidate while quiescent (`Lane::candidate`,
`Lane::settle`, plan section 6.6), records `candidate_submitted`, verifies the
candidate commit in a checkout from the pool's verification reserve (section 7.6,
`Purpose::Verification`, so full worker capacity cannot starve the check), prepares
the merge in a staging lane under one integration lock (`plan/acceptance.rs`),
verifies that exact tree and keeps the staging branch as the pin; the owner's `done`
is legal only at the accept phase read from the journal (`phase_of`; typestate
`Candidate<Submitted>` to `Candidate<Verified>` to `Integration<Verified>`),
publishes under the generation check (`publish` in `lane/settle.rs`: a fast-forward,
or the ref alone with the checkout following on every path the user did not touch,
read with `-uall` so an untracked directory's files count as the user's, run from
the repository root whatever the session cwd, and any other fast-forward failure, an
index lock or an unconcluded merge, returned for a retry) and commits `accepted {
candidate, parent_base, integrated, published, how, verdicts }` with the transition;
a moved parent records `integration_stale` and prepares again, bounded per call, and
a later `done` at the stale phase prepares again from the verified candidate; a
second `submit` of one token replays a settled refusal, joins the flight or adopts a
dead claim as `done` does; `fail`, `drop`, `supersede` and the reap record
`Retained`, `Discarded` or `MergeFailed` before the lane and slot are released (a
merge failure keeps the branch and the slot until the resolved candidate is submitted
again), a lane that cannot settle stays on its record with its slot held, and
`delete` refuses a worktree without a disposition; the probe loop reports a stuck
child once per episode and an earlier due time wakes it (stage F0d of
`docs/plans/2026-09-12-yi-operating-system.md`)

## Why

Until F0d `done` on a worktree todo was refused as unavailable (D194) and the only
merge path was the B11 hand-back, which merged in the user's checkout; `reap`
dropped the lane with whatever the child had not committed, so the pool's next
`reset --hard` erased it; the two review rounds found the settle consuming a lane it
could not settle, a stale publication no later `done` could prepare again, the ref-
only publication taken on any fast-forward failure and restoring over a nested
untracked file, pathspecs resolved against a subdirectory cwd after the ref had
moved, and `submit` running one token's checks twice

## Reversible via

Revert to 0.267.0's `AcceptanceUnavailable` refusal on a worktree `done` and the B11
`merge_worktree` hand-back; the journal kinds `candidate_submitted`,
`candidate_verified`, `integration_prepared`, `integration_verified`,
`integration_stale`, `accepted` and `disposition` stay readable as records the
reducer ignores
