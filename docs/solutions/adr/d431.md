# D431: the scene is chosen by one weighted draw at console start, Kali eight parts...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The scene is chosen by one weighted draw at console start, Kali eight parts in ten
and Echo two by default, and the title plays by default in an interactive console,
ending at once on a key

## Why

The owner: "Choose a weighted random startup . customizable. i want kali fold the
most" and "i need to see the animation on startup"

## Reversible via

Set console.titleIntro false, or console.titleWeights to a single scene
