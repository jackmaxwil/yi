# D459: A history hit's snippet keeps the line's own case and a clipped one adds a...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A history hit's snippet keeps the line's own case and a clipped one adds a [...] row
naming the 160-char cap and the address that reads the whole entry (amends D277 and
D279 reply text)

## Why

The snippet was lowercased and clipped with no notice, which the loud-caps law
forbids, and history.grep hits had no address to read

## Reversible via

Restore the lowered snippet and drop the row and the url
