# D443: a login profile may be `kind

Status: accepted

## Decision

A login profile may be `kind: "device-code"`: Yi runs RFC 8628 against the profile's
`device_authorization` and `token` with its `client_id`, polls at the server's
interval (+5 s on `slow_down`) until approval, denial, `expired_token` or
min(`expires_in`, 15 min), stores the token like any OAuth login and refreshes it
with the same locked refresh grant; a profile may name the `api_base` its login
serves; Yi still ships no client id, endpoint or wire identity (D191 holds)

## Why

The owner: "enable login with kimi account"; Kimi Code's only account login is the
RFC 8628 device code (kimi-code packages/oauth/src/types.ts:1-9, oauth.ts:117-210)

## Reversible via

Drop the kind, `device.rs`, `Login::Device` and the
`device_authorization`/`api_base` profile keys
