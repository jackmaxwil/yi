# D322: review rounds block (revises D319's `shadow`)

Status: accepted

## Decision

Review rounds block (revises D319's `shadow`): `pr_review.MODE` is `blocking`, so a
round with a high finding fails the `review` job and `just pr ready` refuses a draft
without two rounds whose last is on its head and not blocked, until a clean round or
`/override <reason>` from an allowed author; medium and low stay advice; `just land`
opens the draft and stops

## Why

Owner: "The review bot should start blocking PRs on negative findings."; the
severity-tier decision makes high the blocking tier

## Reversible via

Set `MODE = "shadow"`
