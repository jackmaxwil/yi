# D618: in the console a press on a tool card or a folded thought opens that one...

Status: accepted

## Decision

In the console a press on a tool card or a folded thought opens that one cell whole
where it stands and the next press folds it, and no pane opens beside the chat; a
shell command's head is kept whole and cut only past its card's rows, a long result
row takes the rows its card has left, and a card keeps the first 80 and last 40
lines of its result (revises the press-opens-a-pane clause of the one row budget)

## Why

The owner: "tool calls are being cut off prematurely. i think it works by a hard
char limit. i think it should be more dynamic based on available tui screen real
estate. i should be able to expand tool calls inline with a mouse click, both the
tool call invocation itself and the result", and "opening thinking opens on the
right side in its own panel. i dont want it in its own panel. should be expanded
inline"

## Reversible via

Reversible by restoring `open_thought` and the 120-character cut in `arg_summary`'s
callers
