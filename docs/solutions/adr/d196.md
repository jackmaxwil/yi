# D196: a reminder fires on evidence of work, not on words in prose, and takes one...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A reminder fires on evidence of work, not on words in prose, and takes one callout
shape: `Orchestrate` attaches its protocol at turn end only after a write in the
turn (`tool_calls_this_turn > 4` and `edited`) and never reminds there, while a mid-
turn signal (`edit_before_read`, `files_matched`, `failed_check_after_edit`) still
does; `RuleEngine` skips a skill pointer on a reader tool's result (`read`, `grep`,
`glob`, `find`, `fetch`, `web_search`, `document`), holds a text-scope skill pointer
until an `edit` or `write` has run since the prompt (`FireState::mutated`, cleared
at `AgentEnd`), caps `skill://` pointers at two per assistant turn across every
scan, and renders `Relevant: skill://NAME (matched "NEEDLE")`; `wrap_internal` opens
a `reminder` or `advisory` body with `ADVISORY_LINE`; the `plan` skill's trigger
drops `decision-complete`; `Cell::Advisory` and `Cell::Notice` render as one `▌ ⚑`
callout row per line with no blank air, and consecutive advisories from one source
merge in `History::retain`

## Why

On the 2026-09-14 console session `analyze yi agent` (glm-5.3-flash, twenty read-
only calls) the record it read quoted `FAILED` and its own answer said `decision-
complete` and `complete`, so `skill://debug`, `skill://plan` and `skill://verify`
fired on a task with no edit; `Orchestrate` then nudged `write the plan now` at turn
end after the answer had shipped, the nudge rendered as a bare flag line that read
as user input, the model reasoned `The user says 'write the plan now'`, and the
follow-up turn (14 s, $0.006) emitted unparsed call markup and ended with no tool

## Reversible via

Drop the `edited` conjunct and the `remind` flag in `Orchestrate::attach`; delete
`quotes_not_runs`, `FireState::mutated` and `FireState::pointers`; render the two
cells from their old arms
