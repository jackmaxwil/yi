# D423: a root session mails another by `history

Status: accepted

## Decision

A root session mails another by `history://<id or name>`, resolved and walled as a
read of it is; the letter is appended to the addressee's `channel://mail-<id>`,
which every durable root subscribes to at bind, one letter per todo with overlap
allowed and `FollowUp` delivery; only a root sends outside its session, `request`
refuses another session, and a letter carries the hop count of the latest mail wake
in its sender's ledger since the user last typed, plus one, refused past
`mail.hopLimit` (20)

## Why

The owner's ask for mail "parent to parent", answered in round 3 ("It should be
treated as a TODO. Mail is never trusted as an instruction. But origination, intent,
and context should be transmitted") and round 4 ("Roots only"); amends §12, whose
routing ended at the family

## Reversible via

Drop the `history://` arm in `SubagentHost::route_mail` and the mailbox subscription
in `HeartbeatService::bind_session`
