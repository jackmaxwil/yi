# D610: `desktop/` takes Rune as one full git revision of the owner's fork and...

Status: accepted

## Decision

`desktop/` takes Rune as one full git revision of the owner's fork and `yi-types`
and `yi-browser` by path, and is gated on a Mac by its own `desktop/justfile`; Yi's
cargo, CI and Rust guardrails read none of it, and the repository-wide gates it
trips take four named exemptions: two address substitutions in
`scripts/mirror/replace.txt`, two journey fixture paths in `.gitguardian.yaml`, two
blobs in the blob allowlist and two spelling skips in `.codespellrc` (the app's
build output and its recorded wire frames)

## Why

The app's lock holds 461 packages, most of them Rune's, against Yi's budget of 23
direct and 174 transitive, so it cannot join Yi's workspace, and no forge runner is
a Mac; the mockup's sample network is private-range, Rune's script format names a
tapped element `key`, which the secret scan reads as a key, the app icon and a
recorded kernel transcript are past 512 KB, and recorded frames are cut mid-word

## Reversible via

Delete `desktop/`, `.ruler/098-desktop.md` and its AGENTS.md block, §17.5 and the
§1.2 words this change adds, the two `replace.txt` lines, the two
`.gitguardian.yaml` paths, the two allowlist lines and the two `.codespellrc` skips
