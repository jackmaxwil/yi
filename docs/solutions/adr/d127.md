# D127: every ratchet moves through one verb, and an ADR is rendered from its row

Status: accepted

## Decision

Every ratchet moves through one verb, and an ADR is rendered from its row: `just
ratchet` runs the comment budget beside test LOC, crate size and the schema lock,
then calls the `binary_ratchet` that already existed for `just commit` — a
`scripts/build_dist.sh` build and the binary baseline in its own `Ratchet:` commit,
skippable with `--no-binary`; `just adr <N>` renders `docs/solutions/adr/dN.md` from
the D-row's three cells and files the index line

## Why

The landing sequence after a merge with main was hand-run and every step of it had
gone red: the comment budget and the binary baseline were outside the one verb that
claims to ratchet, `check_binary_size.py` was the only gate with no `--update`, and
D125 landed with neither an ADR nor an index line because writing one was a separate
act of prose. Generating the ADR from the row is what CLAUDE.md already asks for —
regenerated from the row rather than hand-drifted — and it is why the growth memo
stays hand-written: `check_growth.py` prints the number, but the sentence about what
was weighed for deletion is not derivable

## Reversible via

Drop the two verbs; `scripts/adr.py` and the `binary_ratchet` call are additive, and
every baseline still has its own `--update`
