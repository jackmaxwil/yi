# D412: every kitty placement and the delete of the image it replaced ride the...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Every kitty placement and the delete of the image it replaced ride the synchronized
update of the frame that moves them, and a placement opens no update of its own; a
console chat reveals streamed text at the configured `tui.pace` and its loop wakes
for the reveal

## Why

The orb, the console's avatars and its notebook image were placed after the frame's
update closed, so a presented frame showed them on their old rows over the text that
had moved (19 frames in one measured console reply, 40 to 57 in solo), and
`place_nth`'s own `?2026` could not nest inside a frame because the mode is a flag
in Ghostty and kitty; console chats were built with pace 0 and the loop's wake
omitted the reveal, so a reply painted each network burst whole: 15.5 visible
updates a second, a median 65 ms apart, against 80.8 and 11.8 ms after on the same
stream

## Reversible via

Restore `place_nth`'s bracket, call `orb::tick`, `logos::tick` and the console's
`place_*` after the update closes, and build console chats with `pace: 0`
