# D126: the TUI paints streamed text through a reveal cursor

Status: accepted

## Decision

`yi-tui::reveal::Reveal` sits between the arrived snapshot and the painted tail,
one cursor per stream (prose, thought). Per loop tick it spends the elapsed time
(clamped to 50 ms) at `max(backlog / gap, 40 cps)`, where `gap` is an EMA of the
provider's inter-burst gap clamped to 150-1200 ms. A sentence end costs six
letters and a clause end two and a half, so the pause reads and compresses when
the reveal is behind; the cursor never rests before a combining mark or inside a
run of `*_` `` ` `` `~`. An assistant `MessageEnd` and every event queued behind it
wait in `Pacing.held` until both cursors reach the edge, at which point the tail
drains at the 150 ms horizon with a 240 cps floor. Stable-prefix commits and the
live render read the shown prefix, never the arrival. `tui.pace` scales the rate
and `0` restores paint-on-arrival, which the headless drive uses unless it
records.

## Why

Providers that batch (OpenRouter's routed backends among them) flush a paragraph
every second, and `MessageUpdate` already arrives as a full-text snapshot that the
16 ms scheduler painted at once, so the screen was an exact trace of the
provider's burst shape.

Rate proportional to backlog over one measured gap was chosen over a fixed
characters-per-second (which stalls or lags without bound, depending on the
provider) and over interpolation toward a predicted arrival deadline (a steady
rate that stops dead when the provider is late). With the rate a low-pass filter
over arrival, the lag settles at about one burst - the least buffer that can
smooth a periodic burst - and a late burst decays into the floor instead of
stopping. Holding `MessageEnd` behind the cursor rather than snapping keeps the
end of every message paced and keeps a tool row from committing above prose the
reader has not seen.

Word-level easing, an eased curve and per-provider profiles were weighed and left
out: the EMA adapts on its own. The console's embedded chat passes `0` for now;
its 50 ms poll and its own options are a separate wiring.

## Reversible via

Set `pace: 0` at the three call sites, or delete `crates/tui/src/reveal.rs` and
the `Pacing` struct: `arrive` then assigns the snapshot and commits it as before.
