# D477: yi-orb depends on yi-types, for the one shared encoder

Status: accepted

## Decision

Yi-orb depends on yi-types, for the one shared encoder

## Why

Haul DUPHELP: three standard base64 encoders and a URL-safe one sat in four crates,
and the orb was the one crate that could not share

## Reversible via

Reversible via a private encoder in yi-orb
