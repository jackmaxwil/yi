# D447: Verification runs up to max(1, min(4, slots/4)) acceptances at once and a...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Verification runs up to max(1, min(4, slots/4)) acceptances at once and a waiter
waits until the verifier's deadline

## Why

Owner: "Fix bugs, keep design"; one permit with a 30 s wait bounced every concurrent
worktree acceptance whose check outlasted it

## Reversible via

Restore VERIFICATION_RESERVE 1 and RESERVE_WAIT 30 s
