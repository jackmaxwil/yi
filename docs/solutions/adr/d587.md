# D587: The red ladder

Status: accepted

## Decision

The red ladder: the refused verdicts on a todo's attempt pick a rung from
`plan/ladder.rs::LADDER` under `plan.done_refusal_cap` (default 3, held on
`Verifier` so the done gate, the cap block and the readmission check read one
value); below cap − 1 `done` runs its check, at cap − 1 it is refused before the
check until a `split` of the todo or an `append` whose `for_task` names it, the
refusal carrying both calls' literal shape; the cap-th still derives `block{on:
User}`, now with a stock three-option ask stamped like any other, and only an
`unblock` carrying the user's reply to an ask readmits; a `retry` opens a fresh
count unless the attempt it ends reached the cap, whose count the next attempt
carries, so a fail and retry buys no fresh free check; a readmission buys one claim,
spent at the next `verification_requested`; the engine decides it live and journals
it as `readmit` in the record's `extra`, so a replay never reads the lever; `split`
adds one to four children `<label>-split-<n>` once per parent, never under a child,
keeps the streak, and the shared completion validator holds the parent on every road
to done (`done`, `accepted_by_user`, `set`, `reconcile`) until each child is done or
dropped; a split child's structural rung offers the targeted `append` alone, and
`admit` refuses its split before anything applies; a `split` or targeted `append`
that buys no claim is refused (amends D194 and D216's section 13.1 cap and D212's
per-attempt count, which a retry past the cap no longer resets)

## Why

Three claims on a red product cost three checker runs before anything asked the
model to change its approach, and the imported design reset the streak on a split
and left the cap's ask-less block with no exit a user could reach: no surface mints
the user principal for an `unblock`, and a fail and retry re-armed two free checks

## Reversible via

Delete `plan/ladder.rs`, the gate and admission calls in `plan/done.rs`,
`Op::Split`, `SplitSpec` and `Append.for_task`; `refuse` counts refused verdicts as
it did and the cap's block, without its question, stays the only rung
