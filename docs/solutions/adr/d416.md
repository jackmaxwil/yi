# D416: every ⌘ chord the console decodes is one neither Ghostty 1.3.1 nor kitty...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Every ⌘ chord the console decodes is one neither Ghostty 1.3.1 nor kitty 0.49.2
binds by default, held by a test over both terminals' default keys rather than a
runtime filter: review ⌘R to ⌘⇧R and find ⌘L to ⌘⇧L (kitty binds cmd+r and cmd+l),
new tab ⌘⇧T to ⌘⇧O, and the ⌘1..9, ⌘D and ⌘⇧D decodes go, so rail slots and splits
answer only ⌥ and ⌘\

## Why

The earlier move to ⌘R and ⌘L was checked against Ghostty alone, and a hint-time
filter left decodes no terminal delivers; a test that fails on a held key keeps both
the decoder and the hints honest

## Reversible via

Restore the arms in keys::super_chord and TERMINAL_HELD with Chord::key's filter
