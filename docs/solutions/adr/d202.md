# D202: transcript text wraps at one width, the terminal's less the two-column...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Transcript text wraps at one width, the terminal's less the two-column gutter, live
and committed alike: `App::content_width` drops the 100-column `MEASURE` cap, and
`live_lines` and `run_reflow` read `content_width` instead of computing their own

## Why

On the 2026-09-15 console session over `yi` a thought paragraph streamed at the full
width of a 190-column window, then narrowed to 100 columns and grew taller the
moment its newline committed it, because the live tail never had the cap the commit
path applied; a resize replayed scrollback uncapped too, so the same paragraph read
at two widths depending on when it was drawn

## Reversible via

Restore `MEASURE` in `content_width`; the live tail and the reflow keep reading
`content_width`, so the cap applies everywhere at once
