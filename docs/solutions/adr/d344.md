# D344: a plan-tool key the op does not read is left out and named, not refused...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A plan-tool key the op does not read is left out and named, not refused (amends
D194): `plan::natural` drops a top-level or todo-level key that no op reads for this
op, names every dropped key in one `note:` line of the reply (`<op> reads no <keys>;
left out, they stay in this call's arguments`), and the call lands; three kinds of
key still refuse: one that carries a hint saying where it goes (`check`, `accept`,
`on`, `output`, a todo's `todos`), one a single insertion, deletion, substitution or
neighbour swap away from a key the tool reads for any op (`contarct`, `outpt`), and
`actor`; the `delegate` alias moves from `declare::todo_of` into the same pass.
Owner, choosing between more per-shape rounds and a rule: "Drop it, say so"

## Why

Five rounds of twelve dogfood sessions (#982) each found new keys at a steady rate
(round 5: 16 of 106 plan calls refused for shape alone, most of them keys like
`description`, `question`, `cause` or a `todo` inside a todo), and a refusal for a
key nothing reads costs a full retry for no safety; D194's reason for refusing, a
misspelled `contract` landing a todo on the unverified path, is kept by the one-edit
guard, which the dogfood fixture's two misspelling cases pin

## Reversible via

Delete `unknown` and `one_edit` from `plan/natural.rs` and restore
`refuse_unknown`'s invariant comment; the `delegate` rename goes back into
`declare::todo_of`
