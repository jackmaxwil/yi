# D344: the lever ceilings of `family.cap` and `family.max_children` are 128 (were...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The lever ceilings of `family.cap` and `family.max_children` are 128 (were 32 and
16); their defaults stay 16 and 8, so only an eval run's `YI_LEVERS` file raises
them, and `family.depth` keeps its 1..=3 range; plan dispatch width stays
`min(family.max_children, plan.width_max)`, so plans do not widen past
`plan.width_max` (at most 16)

## Why

The owner's swarm dogfood of 2026-09-30: "lets raise subagent cap to 128 with depth
3"; a 20-30 wide fan-out, and a 128 wide one, had no lever value that admitted it

## Reversible via

Restore the 2..=16 and 4..=32 ranges in `levers.rs` and `evals/levers/levers.json`
