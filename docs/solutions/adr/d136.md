# D136: a tool call is a card, and the outcome is read from its chrome

Status: accepted

## Decision

A tool call is a card, and the outcome is read from its chrome: `yi_tui::card` draws
a `│` rail in the outcome's colour (accent running, amber awaiting, green done, red
failed), the head in a per-tool hue, the counts as chips right-aligned to the
measure (`412 lines`, `⏎ 1`, `+1 −1`, elapsed amber past a second) and body rows
inset on the tinted ground; kernel cells and explored runs render through the same
`card`

## Why

One muted grey for every finished call and `exit 0` spelled out made outcomes
unreadable at a glance; a rail and a number carry the outcome at 16 colours and at
true colour alike

## Reversible via

Delete `card.rs` and put the glyph, digest row and `· exit N` back in
`ToolCell::lines`
