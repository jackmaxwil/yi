# D410: Open in runs an app the person lists in config.json (`open

Status: accepted

## Decision

Open in runs an app the person lists in config.json (`open: [{name, command}]`,
`{path}` standing for the message saved as markdown under ~/.yi/exports), spawned
detached by the daemon on a `_yi/open{name, text}` request; there is no built-in app
list

## Why

The owner asked for "Open in: submenu: Finder, Cursor, Zed, Preview" and chose "Apps
listed in config" over a hard-coded per-OS list, which would break "Yi should be
generic and extensible, not opinionated here"; the console may not spawn

## Reversible via

Drop the `open` config key, the `_yi/open` handler and its spawn site, and the
menu's Open in row
