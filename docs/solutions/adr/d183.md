# D183: done needs evidence, not a typography

Status: accepted

## Decision

Done needs evidence, not a typography: `Op::Done` with no evidence or blank evidence
is refused as before (`TodoError::NoEvidence`) and any other evidence closes the
item; `evidence_shaped`, `TodoError::EvidenceShape` and the shaped branch go; the
stop intercept's done move reads ``evidence=`<command>` <output line>``, as the
tool's `next:` line does, instead of `evidence=<the check that passed>`; a `set`
still cannot close an item, and `extract.py`'s `evidence_shape_refused` now counts
that refusal alone (amends D149; reverses 0.195.0's shape rule)

## Why

0.195.0 asked for a command in backticks and twelve characters of its output outside
them; on row 0028 that refused 95 of 126 `done` calls, most with the output quoted
in backticks too or an honest `cmd → output` with none, a check that prints `ok`
could never close, 97 of 321 turns held nothing but refused dones (31% of the run's
cost), and a search-loop attempt whose checker was green at turn 9 spent turns 9-113
on 29 rephrasings before it dropped the item; the refusal never named the twelve
characters, and the intercept taught a form the rule refused; `done_without_check`,
the signal the rule guarded, read 0 in every row from 0015 through 0025, before the
rule existed as after (the 2026-09-10 harness audit, G1, T1)

## Reversible via

Restore `evidence_shaped`, `EvidenceShape` and the shaped branch from 0.224.0; the
hint's wording stands alone
