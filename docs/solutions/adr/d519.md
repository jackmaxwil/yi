# D519: the autofixer tells a red base and a person's call apart from a failure, and...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The autofixer tells a red base and a person's call apart from a failure, and settles
a lone binary overrun itself

## Why

A week of its ledger held 25 failures of which 8 were main's own red test reported
as flaky, 9 were calls no model could make, and 8 attempts at $8.80 sent a size no
edit could reach to a model; the 45 KB role prefix it kept measuring was its own
`TMPDIR` spelled through the checkout with `..`

## Reversible via

Restore the `RuntimeError` raises, remove the `base-red` and `handoff` verdicts and
the `raise_binary` call in scripts/pr_autofix.py, and put the `..` back in
autofix.yml
