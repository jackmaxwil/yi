# D565: a head is reviewed once and one clean round promotes it (revises D322 and...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A head is reviewed once and one clean round promotes it (revises D322 and D332's two
rounds): a head a round has read whole, clean or blocked, is never read again,
whatever triggers it, a lost lens is the only reread and asks just the lenses still
owed with the earlier round's findings kept and no refuter paid for again, and `just
pr ready`, `just pr merge` and the sweep's promotion take one round on the head

## Why

The bot paid for a second full round on an unchanged head: #1888, #1977, #1830 and
#1974 each ran two or three rounds on one sha ($0.30 to $1.09 each), from the second
clean round a draft needed, a review job that called the round twice, `--again`
skipping every reason but a blocked head, and a void erasing which lens was lost

## Reversible via

Restore `wanted=2` in `skip_reason`, the two-round clause of `ready_problems` and
the blocked-head rule of `--again` in scripts/pr_review.py
