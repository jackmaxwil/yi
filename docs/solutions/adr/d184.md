# D184: a todo move's result names what moved

Status: accepted

## Decision

A todo move's result names what moved: `start`, `done`, `drop`, `block`, `unblock`,
`append` and `rm` return the header, the checklist rows the op changed or added and
the `next:` lines, and leave the rows it did not touch out (`text::render_change`
over `Applied.before`, the list as the op found it); `init`, `set` and `view` still
return the whole list, as does a move that empties it; `yi todo`, the HUD and the
environment line are unchanged (amends D137, D155)

## Why

On row 0028 todo results were 46,910 of 76,322 tool-result characters (61.5%), a
median of 450-540 characters for a one-line state change, and about 364k resident
tokens (6% of the billed context), since nothing compacts a result; nothing parses
the result (the intercept, the nudge and the prelude recompute from the store), the
environment block already carries the counts each turn, and `view` lists the whole
list on demand (the 2026-09-10 harness audit, T3)

## Reversible via

`TodoTool::run` renders every op with `text::render` again; `Applied.before` and
`render_change` go with it
