# D488: A bash job poll sees only its own session's jobs, and the poll can kill a...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A bash job poll sees only its own session's jobs, and the poll can kill a job and
show its output so far

## Why

A bare poll returned the newest job in the process and a model could not stop a job
it started

## Reversible via

Reversible via latest() and dropping kill
