# D438: the console's full sidebar groups sessions by workspace under a PINNED...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The console's full sidebar groups sessions by workspace under a PINNED section, in
place of D276's needs you / working / idle sections and the separate workspaces
block (amends D276)

## Why

The owner asked to "organize by workspace + pinned. Copy the claude code sidebar
organization and layout" and chose "Needs-you first, then recent (Recommended)"
inside a group (#1376); the inbox mixed every root's sessions into one need-ranked
list, and the workspaces block was a filter beside it rather than the list's own
structure

## Reversible via

Restore the section headers in `sidebar_lines`, the need-then-recency sort across
roots in `visible_rows`, `render_roots` with its `root_filter`, and drop `groups`,
`folded` and `_yi/pin`; a ledger written meanwhile keeps `pinnedMs`, which an older
daemon reads as an unknown field
