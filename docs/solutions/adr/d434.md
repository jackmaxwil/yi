# D434: a blocked session's rail avatar shows one still frame of its Listening loop...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A blocked session's rail avatar shows one still frame of its Listening loop instead
of playing it, and leaves the console's wake schedule; a working session's avatar
still plays its state loop at 15 fps (amends D276)

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-12: "Invisible + freeze waiting"; every avatar waiting on the
person woke the console fifteen times a second to repaint a row whose words already
say "needs your answer"

## Reversible via

Drop `Motion::still`, which restores the loop
