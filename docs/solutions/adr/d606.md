# D606: A `session/prompt` that arrives mid-turn is queued and answered at once with...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A `session/prompt` that arrives mid-turn is queued and answered at once with
`{messageId, queuePlace}`, and starts-or-queues is one decision under the session's
status lock (amends D351's answer-only-after-insertion for queued prompts)

## Why

A client could not tell a queued prompt from a lost one, and the start-then-queue
pair of calls let a prompt sent between them start ahead of the queue

## Reversible via

Answer queued prompts at insertion again and restore the two-call fallback in
`crates/acp/src/lib.rs`
