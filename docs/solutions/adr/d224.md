# D224: the engine starts ready delegated todos

Status: accepted

## Decision

The engine starts ready delegated todos: `PlanEngine::apply` is `apply_once` and then
one pass of `plan/schedule.rs::dispatch_ready`, which reads the family's journal
without the lease and applies `start` as `Actor::Engine` (journaled `engine`) to each
delegated Pending todo `admitted` lets through, skipping a label with a pending
`spawn_intent` (the repair road's) and one whose start was refused and journaled for
this attempt in this process; what it spawned joins the op's `Outcome.spawned`, a
refusal is one `notices` line the plan tool now prints, and the plan, ready and held
lists are re-derived, so `dispatched` names only what the owner still steps.
`check_actor` admits `Engine` to `start`, `submit`, `done` and `fail`. A child's op, a
`view` and a delegate that hosts nothing (the CLI's `Unhosted`, `Delegate::hosts`)
schedule nothing; the probe tick's `dispatch_ready_all` is the backstop for a resumed
session that issues no op. `block` is the hold and `unblock` the release; there is no
opt-out field and no lever. `Delegate::follow_up` and its "you start each one" steer
are deleted, the plan tool description and doctrine lose the owner-start instruction,
and in the library `Run.launch` adopts an engine-started delegated todo instead of
sending `start`, the shapes settle what the engine starts, a pod holds its declared
arbiter with `block`, and a failed reader is abandoned behind an edge on an open todo
(amends D212: shapes schedule inline `run=` work and observe delegated work; stage G1
of `docs/plans/2026-09-22-the-engine-runs-the-plan.md`)

## Why

The 105 recorded eval sessions of the 0.283.0 confirmation show the model failing
where it must remember a procedure the runtime could execute, and `start` after
`append` was the first of them: two F0e parents read "ready to start" as work already
started (#470), and the fix that followed named the step instead of removing it; an
admitted delegated todo has exactly one legal next move, so the engine makes it

## Reversible via

Delete `plan/schedule.rs`, the `Engine` actor and `Delegate::hosts`, fold
`apply_once` back into `apply`, restore `Delegate::follow_up` and its steer from
0.286.0, and `Run.launch` sends `start` again
