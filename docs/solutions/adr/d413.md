# D413: a chat pane that follows the turn rolls rows in at most one a frame, one...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A chat pane that follows the turn rolls rows in at most one a frame, one more per 24
rows it is behind, by pinning its window to the transcript cell at its top and
moving the window's bottom toward the column's

## Why

A measured console reply moved 2 to 3 rows at once in 17 of 60 moving frames, at
every paragraph and fence boundary, because a blank row and the next row's first
character reveal in one step; the owner chose "Roll in, 1 row/frame" over blocks
appearing at once

## Reversible via

Drop `pane_follow` and paint the last `above` rows of the column again
