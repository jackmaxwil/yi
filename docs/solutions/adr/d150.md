# D150: the real clock is in the environment block

Status: accepted

## Decision

The real clock is in the environment block: `yi ask --deadline <secs>` (the flag the
TUI drive already parsed, no longer refused outside `--headless`) flows through
`RuntimeWiring.deadline` into the per-turn `<environment>` block as `deadline:
<remaining>s of <total>s`, counted from the session's start, a runtime fact that
never enters the transcript or the cached prefix; the harbor adapter passes 28,800 ×
`EVAL_TIMEOUT_MULT` (harbor's `AgentContext` is metrics-out and never tells the
agent its timeout), `evals/run.py` passes the task's `timeoutSec`, the journeys pass
their timeout, and identity.md names the line and says to land the answer before it
reaches zero

## Why

Every v4 instruction says 28,800 s and the ledger's trial gets 3,600; row 0017's
music-harmony attempt spent 45 minutes and one 77k-token turn without knowing it was
near the wall, and harbor's kill is the only clock the run had (issue #269). A flag
over an env var keeps `env_vars.json` unchanged and reuses the parser that exists

## Reversible via

Drop the wiring field and the line; the flag returns to drive-only
