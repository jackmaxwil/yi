# D558: reclaim decides a cut in tokens alone

Status: accepted

## Decision

Reclaim decides a cut in tokens alone: the tokens a batch drops, times the requests
so far, must exceed ten reads (REWRITE_READS) of the tokens after its first result,
and the batch must drop 2,000 tokens, the rewrite counted at twenty reads for an
hour-cache write, on every route whatever its catalog prices; a lapsed cache still
cuts at once (amends the reclaim decision of #1149)

## Why

A route whose catalog prices cache at $0 never paid, so a subscription lane cut
nothing in 232 sessions at a 218k-token median, while its cache reads and rewrites
cost quota the price test could not see

## Reversible via

Restore the price comparison in yi_runtime's reclaim::reason
