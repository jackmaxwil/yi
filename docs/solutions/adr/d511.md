# D511: `just test-quick` is an opt-in local loop that excludes tests measured at 5...

Status: accepted

## Decision

`just test-quick` is an opt-in local loop that excludes tests measured at 5 seconds
or more, while `just check`, prepush and CI keep the complete suite; `just slow-
tests` regenerates the exact binary-and-test filter from a fresh nextest JUnit
report

## Why

The owner approved skipping the measured slow tests for the inner loop: "Yes
(Recommended)"

## Reversible via

Remove the quick profiles and recipes; the full suite is unchanged
