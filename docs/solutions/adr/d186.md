# D186: the v4 calibrated slice is chosen by a sweep, not by cost

Status: accepted

## Decision

The v4 calibrated slice is chosen by a sweep, not by cost:
`evals/drivers/tbv4_sweep.sh` runs a list of host-feasible tasks as one harbor job
under `watch.py` (a trial stopped at $1 or 180 turns, the job at $20 spent, 7 h 45
min of wall or 8 GB free; images no container uses pruned by id), and the slice is
the tasks whose one-draw partial credit sat in [0.2, 0.8] with at least five scored
units, nearest 0.5 first, twelve of them (`evals/drivers/tbv4_slice.txt`, from row
0055); `tbv4_baseline.sh` and its six tasks stay byte-stable

## Why

The six-task slice read 3/18 through forty decisions and could not move, and pass@1
on the sweep is 3/38, so a pass rate on a dozen tasks cannot see a harness change at
this model; partial credit can, and 19 of 38 measured tasks sit mid-band; one job
keeps all three slots busy to the wall where per-task runs wait on their slowest
trial

## Reversible via

Run `tbv4_baseline.sh`; the slice is a text file, and a first k=3 row that reads a
task identical three times swaps it for the next band task
