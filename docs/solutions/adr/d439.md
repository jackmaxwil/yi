# D439: A new crate `yi-browser` drives the user's installed Chrome or Chromium...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A new crate `yi-browser` drives the user's installed Chrome or Chromium headless
over `--remote-debugging-pipe`; the hidden `yi __fds` subcommand moves the child's
stdin and stdout to fds 3 and 4 with rustix `dup2` and execs Chrome, and
`Page.startScreencast` PNG frames go to kitty as `f=100` with no decode, on two
image ids per pane starting at 0xF00000

## Why

The owner asked to "port terminal-browser to rust. new create. use kitty graphics
protocol to embed if applicable" and to "use installed chrome"; terminal-browser's
page source is a patched Electron Rust cannot reach, `forbid(unsafe_code)` refuses a
`pre_exec`, and a pipe needs no websocket crate and opens no port

## Reversible via

Delete `crates/browser`, `PaneContent::Browser`, the `__fds` fast path and the
`browser.path` key
