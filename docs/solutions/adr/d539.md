# D539: A reminder time naming no offset is wall time in the local zone, and an...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A reminder time naming no offset is wall time in the local zone, and an offset is
honoured (amends D283's `at <ISO>`, read as UTC with offsets refused)

## Why

GLM 5.3 flash wrote `-08:00` on every timed reminder, and the refusal sent it to a
naive time that fired at once or to a blocker nothing fires

## Reversible via

Read a naive `at` as UTC again in `parse_local_ms`
