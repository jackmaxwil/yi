# D387: a run's `convert_to_llm` cuts tool results out of the model's view, never...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A run's `convert_to_llm` cuts tool results out of the model's view, never out of the
session: a text-only result at least 1,200 chars long and 5 requests old, other than
`ask_user`, `plan`, `todo` and the newest `read` of a path, is cut once the tokens
the cut drops (at least 2,000) × the cache-read price × the requests so far exceed
the tokens after its first result, measured over the view with earlier cuts at
placeholder size, × (cache-write − cache-read price); picks are dropped from the
front until the rest pays; an uncached route prices both at input, a route that
reads cache at a rate its catalog omits never pays, and a one-hour write costs twice
input; the cache has lapsed when the newest real reply (not a synthesized abort or
error) is older than the TTL its cache diagnostic names; a session without a `read`
tool cuts nothing; a `custom{reclaim}` entry names the cut results by tool call id,
text hash (call ids repeat: GLM's leaked calls are `leak-0` in every message) and
entry id, and from then on each renders as one `[reclaimed: <call> → none of N chars
kept; …; get it: read("history://self/<entry>")]` line, or one saying it is not in
the session's store; the cache fold resets at that entry, as at a compaction

## Why

Replaying the $87 Opus session the rule nets 4-5% and the $17 GLM 5.3 session 8-10%,
while a watermark that cut every turn lost $316-466, since each cut rewrites the
cache after it; the owner chose "On now" over a switch held off until a paid A/B;
thinking blocks stay, since Opus on OpenRouter does not replay them

## Reversible via

Build the run's `convert_to_llm` from `yi_context::convert_to_llm` alone again; the
entries stay, unread
