# D173: the ledger row says what it could not measure

Status: accepted

## Decision

The ledger row says what it could not measure: `evals/axes.py` prints D140's pass@k
CI whenever a task has at least two attempts, `±0.0` included; a harbor trial's
`timedOut` is `AgentTimeoutError` alone, `verifierUnmeasured` marks a verifier that
started and returned no result (its own timeout, or one harbor dropped under an
earlier agent timeout), and any other exception or a non-zero run.py `exit` is
`errored`; the row gains `timeouts` (`agent/verifier/error` counts) and `partials`
(`Σpassed/Σtests` over each verifier's `ctrf.json` summary, then the mean
`partial_score` of any `trace_results.json`) on the right

## Why

A CI of exactly 0.0 was dropped as falsy, so rows 0023 and 0025, where every task
passed all or none of its attempts, printed no width and the zero read as no CI at
all (the 2026-09-10 harness audit, E1); photonic cc3HUqA and RVX4Qtj timed out,
their verifiers overran the 300 s ceiling and harbor kept the agent timeout, so both
scored 0.0 exactly like a verified failure (E2); five of the six slice verifiers
write pytest tallies and heat-pump a partial score, and the ledger transcribed them
by hand (E3); on the 2026-09-09 slice archive the two cells read `4/2/0` and
`222/276 0.53`

## Reversible via

Drop the two cells and the four per-trial fields and restore the truthy test on the
width; rows written with the cells stay readable, since columns are additive
