# D191: two time-limit hits in four bash calls ask for a new method, not a longer limit

Status: accepted

## Decision

Two time-limit hits in four bash calls ask for a new method, not a longer limit:
`BashTool` keeps the last four calls that ran as bits, and a call hits when Yi's
timeout killed it or when it exited 124 with a segment running `timeout [OPTION]...
DURATION` for at least DURATION (a wrapped command that exits 124 by itself ends
sooner, and an exit 124 with no wrapper is not a hit); a hit with another in the
window drops the `pass timeout_secs up to` clause and adds `[N of the last 4 bash
calls hit their time limit; change the method, not the limit: bound the work (a
smaller input, a sample, an early exit), vectorize it, or run it in the background
(nohup CMD > out.log 2>&1 &) and read the log in a later call]`; a refused call, a
job poll and a backgrounded command leave the window alone, and each session builds
its own `BashTool`, so the window spans its prompts

## Why

On the 2026-09-11 v4 sweep layout-config-recreation's cv2 search was killed at 600 s
and then ran into its own 590 s `timeout` twice and its 480 s one once (exit 124,
which `timedOut` never saw), and ctr-optimization's polling loops (25 s asleep a
round) ran to 600 s twice around one that finished in 203 s; the search-category
streak first proposed here fired on none of the 999 bash calls, since no sweep
timeout was a grep or a find and the finished poll reset it; replayed over the 997
bash results that reached the trigger, this one fires on 2 of 44 trials, at ctr-
optimization call 27 and layout-config-recreation call 13, and in none of the seven
trials with a single hit (builds, servers, training runs, a VNC proxy under a 60 s
`timeout`); legacy-utility-triage's exit 124 with no wrapper is not a hit; that
replay read the transcripts outside the repository, and the committed tests replay
invented bash results of the same shapes

## Reversible via

Drop `recent_ceilings`, `ceiling_nudge` and the section; every timeout reads `pass
timeout_secs up to 600` again
