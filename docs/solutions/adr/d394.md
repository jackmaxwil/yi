# D394: a drive script's `snap <name>` step keeps the frame drawn at that step as...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A drive script's `snap <name>` step keeps the frame drawn at that step as
`snap-<name>.cast` and `snap-<name>.txt` in the frames dir, and `scripts/tui_see.py`
diffs two runs' snaps into the cell diff (row text and per-cell style changes), the
changed regions and 1:1 crops an agent reads in that order; the cast is the ledger
of a terminal run and every picture of it is a view rendered on demand; nothing in
this pipeline gates a build

## Why

The owner wants agents (Claude Code, Yi workers) to see a TUI change ("Agents seeing
their changes"), frame dumps cannot show how a change looks, and the owner ruled
"Never fail, just show" because a picture gate reddens on font and driver
differences

## Reversible via

Drop the `Snap` step, `write_snap`, `scripts/tui_see.py` and its fixtures under
`scripts/proof/tui_see/`
