# D318: a PR opens as a draft against a filled template

Status: accepted

## Decision

A PR opens as a draft against a filled template: `just pr open` titles it `WIP:
<subject>`, Forgejo's draft marker, and the title rule judges the subject after that
prefix; `just pr ready N` takes it off; the body owes every section of
`.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md` (`check_pr_metadata.REQUIRED`), each present,
non-empty and free of template comments

## Why

30 of 80 merged PR bodies kept a placeholder and passed; the last 80 merged PRs had
one formal review

## Reversible via

Revert `title_errors` to `subject_errors` and stop prefixing
