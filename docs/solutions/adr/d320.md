# D320: the fixer is a fresh `yi ask --auto` on a same-repo draft, fed the last...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The fixer is a fresh `yi ask --auto` on a same-repo draft, fed the last round's high
and medium findings; the host discards a change that touched the gates, the hooks or
the probes; it pushes once per round and only with `YI_REVIEW_FIX=1`; nothing merges

## Why

Owner: "Fresh fixer always"; a fixer that edits its judge has none

## Reversible via

Delete `cmd_fix`
