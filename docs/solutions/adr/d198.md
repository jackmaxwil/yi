# D198: a collapsed tool card shows three result rows and how many it hid

Status: accepted

## Decision

A collapsed tool card shows three result rows and how many it hid: in `normal` and
`thinking` a finished card's body is the first `PREVIEW_ROWS` (3) rows of the typed
body then `… N more lines` from the result's line count (`ToolCell::lines_total`,
read back from the preview and its elision marker), and a prose digest shows only
when there is no preview or the call failed; `verbose` keeps the 12-and-6 preview; a
patch, an `ipython` cell and a `todo` call keep their own bodies; `Chip::exit` is
`None` on zero and `exit N` red otherwise, with a `not found` chip when a zero exit
sits over a `command not found` row; a failed head opens `✗ `, a denied one `⊘ `;
`cell::label` capitalises the tool's name (`→ Read src/lib.rs`) in the summary, the
summary spans, the body's subject and the Explored rows, while `glyph` and
`tool_hue` keep the raw name; `arg_summary` cuts a head at 120 characters, not 60

## Why

On the 2026-09-14 console session `analyze yi agent` every card showed one result
line with no sign of the rest, a green `⏎ 0` sat on every shell row (the one masking
`sh: fgj: command not found` under a pipeline's tail), tool names read `read`
standalone and `Read` grouped, a failed `todo` refusal had the same head as a
success, and commands were cut at 60 characters while the row had room

## Reversible via

Restore `Chip::exit` to a chip on every code, drop `PREVIEW_ROWS`, `lines_total` and
the `label` splits, and put the 60 back
