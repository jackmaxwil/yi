# D83: net src growth is budgeted per version

Status: accepted

## Decision

A version may add 150 net src lines free. Past that, its own changelog row carries a
`growth +N:` memo naming the measured number and what was weighed for deletion; past +2000 the
row must additionally cite the decision row the landing claimed.

`scripts/guardrails/check_growth.py` enforces it. The measurement is `_common.src_files()` —
the identical file set every size ratchet reads — against `scripts/guardrails/baselines/src_loc.json`,
which holds `{version, loc}` and was seeded at the tree 0.82.0 closed with (52,176 lines). The
gate runs outside `--fast`, so the pre-commit hook skips it and the push does not: growth is
priced once per version, in the row a landing writes last. Crossing the free band without
bumping the version fails too, because a memo has nowhere to live until the bump. `--update`
moves the baseline in its own commit, exactly like every other ratchet.

## Rationale

The per-file, per-crate, per-function, per-comment and per-binary ratchets bounded the pieces
while the repository's total slope had no price at all, so growth arrived unexamined and was
rationalized afterwards.

The bands are measured rather than chosen. `--calibrate` walks every version bump in the
history and reports 95 versions, median +171, range -2036..+5978, with 46 under +150 and 49
over, of which 7 clear +2000 — two regimes, so one flat cap would either strangle a landing or
gate nothing, and +150 sits on the seam between them. That means slightly more than half of
this repo's past versions would have owed a memo: the intended price for a repository in heavy
build-out, and a correction to the 17-bump sample that motivated the band, which had reported
an organic median of +35. The three negative deltas in the calibration are artifacts of
0.82.0's duplicate version numbering across the two merged lineages, not deletions.

A memo prices growth instead of forbidding it. The version is the unit because a memo has a row
to live in only once per version, and because a landing that cannot say why its bytes earn
their place should not land.

## Reversal path

Delete `check_growth.py`, its baseline and the aggregator's `run` line. Memos already written
survive as ordinary changelog prose and no code reads them.
