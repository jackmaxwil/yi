# D571: a pane's title label and the rest of its top border move the panel by drag...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A pane's title label and the rest of its top border move the panel by drag even on a
split row, so a resize is grabbed only on the line between panels

## Why

The owner found moving a panel hard because a press on a lower title (which sits on
the split row) always chose a resize, the earlier rule that put the split first

## Reversible via

Restore the split-first check in the snap source and the focus-on-press of a title
