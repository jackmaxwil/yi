# D595: tool results are coloured from a fresh parse at every break in the shown...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Tool results are coloured from a fresh parse at every break in the shown text (the
first row, a line-number gap, an elision row, a clipped row, a parse error) instead
of left plain when the parse cannot be sure

## Why

Most reads start mid-file and every search hit is lifted out of its file, so the
plain rule left nearly every result uncoloured

## Reversible via

Reversible by restoring the line-1 check in the tool card body
