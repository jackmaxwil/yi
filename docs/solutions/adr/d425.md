# D425: a turn ends on an accepted answer

Status: accepted

## Decision

A turn ends on an accepted answer: the `answer` tool
(`yi_runtime::answer::AnswerTool`) runs the stop gate (the todo and plan
`intercept_stop` chain) on the draft before anything is shown, holds a refused draft
as a file under the spill dir for `edit`, and on acceptance the loop's new
`LoopConfig::answer` hook appends the draft as the model's own message and ends the
turn; once answered, a queued message that could not have started a turn on its own
(a host note: not typed input, a task, mail, child news, or a waking delivery) is
dropped, and a plain-text stop is judged once, the next stop ending the turn (amends
the todo stop ladder and the plan ledger stop)

## Why

The stop gate ran after the answer was on screen, so 25 of 310 recent answers (8%)
were reopened by `todo_intercept` and 19 of those went on to tool calls, which the
owner saw as "a final answer, then follow up with more nonsense"

## Reversible via

Drop the `answer` tool and the `answer` hook, and `answer::wire` back to the
coupling's own `prepare_next_turn` and `intercept_stop`; sessions written meanwhile
keep `answer` tool calls and their appended assistant messages, which replay as
ordinary turns
