# D331: the family cap counts every live session, a child with no kernel included,...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The family cap counts every live session, a child with no kernel included, and the
64-held-reader fuse is gone (amends D307): `KernelServiceMap` also keeps a weak
handle on each admitted child session that registered no kernel (a reader today, a
worker once one exists), `KernelServiceMap::live` counts it while anything holds it,
and `SubagentHostOptions::family_live` is that shared map rather than a closure over
its kernels, so admission and a service's respawn register the child and `admit`
refuses the next spawn at `family.cap` live sessions whatever the child's role;
`reader::HELD_CAP` and its refusal in `SubagentHost::reserve` are gone, and a reader
still stands outside the worker cap

## Why

§11 says the family cap binds every standing, but it counted kernels, so a parent
could hold 64 readers beside the cap's 16 full sessions; the owner chose "Count
them" on 2026-09-30, and since `family.cap` is at most 32 the 64-reader fuse could
never refuse first, so the owner chose to delete it (#934)

## Reversible via

Count only the kernels in `KernelServiceMap::live`, and restore `reader::HELD_CAP`
with its refusal in `SubagentHost::reserve`
