# D429: the title is a scalar field sampled into glyph cells and drawn last over...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The title is a scalar field sampled into glyph cells and drawn last over every cell
of the console's frame, a startup animation that ends on its own, not a mode of the
orb

## Why

The orb-morph chain was too grounded in avatars and not fullscreen, and a title
under the chat covered 70% of the pane; a cover needs no matching and runs on every
terminal

## Reversible via

Remove crates/tui/src/title.rs and its call sites; the config keys are additive
