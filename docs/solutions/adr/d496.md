# D496: an ACP worker keeps at most eight idle sessions loaded and unloads the...

Status: accepted

## Decision

An ACP worker keeps at most eight idle sessions loaded and unloads the idlest past
that when another loads; a session with a running turn, queued prompts or steer, a
running child, a running user cell or a kernel whose snapshot is behind or skipped a
name is pinned; an unloaded session's schedule keeps its timer, and a request naming
it or a job coming due for it loads it again, its channel subscriptions due at once

## Why

The daemon kept every session it ever opened for its life, since no client sends
`session/close`; the owner, 2026-10-13: "Reload it on mail"

## Reversible via

Remove `evict_idle`'s call from `adopt`, which restores the keep-everything worker
