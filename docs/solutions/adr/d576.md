# D576: one row budget read off the screen's height sizes both reasoning and tool...

Status: accepted

## Decision

One row budget read off the screen's height sizes both reasoning and tool results: a
live thought streams in a window of a quarter of the rows (4 to 12, never shrinking
while the thought is live) under a `thinking · <elapsed> · <chars>` head, and a
settled thought commits one `thought · <elapsed> · <chars> · ctrl+o` row; a settled
card keeps a sixth of the rows (4 to 12, doubled for a failed call) and shows the
whole result when that is at most one row more; the default and normal modes fold
thoughts, verbose keeps them whole, and the console opens a folded thought beside
the chat on a press (revises the whole-thought default and the 3-row card preview of
§17.3)

## Why

The owner: "i dont want to completely hide reasoning. instead, i want it reasoning
to stream while its live, and then collapse in the scrollback", "show more than 3
lines", "also increase the size/number of tool call lines in TUI" and "use the
algorithm both on tool calls lines and reasoning lines"; the owner rejected model
summaries, so the fold is counted, never written by a model

## Reversible via

Delete `crates/tui/src/budget.rs`, restore `PREVIEW_ROWS = 3` and commit thoughts
whole outside normal mode
