# D617: Yi Desktop is gated on the forge as a Linux build

Status: accepted

## Decision

Yi Desktop is gated on the forge as a Linux build: a `desktop` lane beside `lint`,
`guardrails` and `test` runs the app's format, lint, HAR and test gates, while its
scenes and journeys stay on a Mac

## Why

The forge has no Mac, so 34 bot commits to `desktop/` in three weeks landed
uncompiled and three of them left main's desktop tests red; a macOS guest on the
Intel host could not draw, and the app builds on Linux once `yd-mac` is Mac-only

## Reversible via

Take `desktop` out of the gate matrix in pr.yml and candidate.yml and out of
`GATE_LANES`
