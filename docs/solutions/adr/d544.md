# D544: a server wait past the retry cap, or `x-should-retry

Status: accepted

## Decision

A server wait past the retry cap, or `x-should-retry: false`, ends the turn stamped
`retry refused:` instead of falling back to backoff, `x-should-retry: true` retries
any status, and the loop reads that stamp, a quota and a refusal as final verdicts
however much output they billed (revises the YI_DESIGN retry bullet)

## Why

A `retry-after: 61` was slept out and the same bytes sent again, and a refusal that
billed output tokens was re-run with a steer saying the stream had dropped

## Reversible via

Restore the backoff fallback in `retry::wait_for` and drop the verdict gate in
`stream_retry::nothing_delivered`
