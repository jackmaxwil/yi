# D295: every request's cache breakpoints come from one typed value, and...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Every request's cache breakpoints come from one typed value, and
`LlmContext.transient` is the only place a per-request block can ride (amends D116
and D291): `yi_ai::breakpoints::Breakpoints::build(route, messages, reuse)` fills at
most four slots in prompt order with a TTL that never rises, `Stable` at the end of
tools and system, `PrevTail` on the previous request's tail (the last user-role
message ahead of the last reply, a free exact-position read) and `Tail` on the last
block before `transient`, with `Shared` left for family caching; the type is named
for the providers' own term, a cache breakpoint, and is not related to Yi's plan
primitive; one `encode(breakpoints, dialect, body, origins)` per wire dialect
(`AnthropicBlocks`, `OpenRouterParts`, `Automatic`), matched exhaustively, so
neither a root mark nor a markless request is representable, and `origins` maps each
rendered message to the transformed history index a breakpoint's `Position` names;
the environment block rides `LlmContext.transient`, which every adapter transforms
and renders after `encode` and no position can name, and `LlmContext.schema:
Option<Value>` holds a structured-output schema for the peer's structured outputs
and the prefix key, unread by the adapters yet; the engine of a `CacheRoute`
(`Breakpoint {slots, hour}` for the Claude family and OpenAI's explicit mode,
`Snapshot` for Gemini, which keeps `Stable` alone, `Prefix` for the rest, whose
breakpoints are free) is derived from the transport and the model id, never a
catalog flag, so a wrong prior moves a breakpoint or a TTL and never removes one;
`Reuse::of` reads the typed request (`tool_choice: none` is a final, no tool table
is a one-shot, the rest loop) and only a loop request marks its tail, since the
other two tails are never read again; the direct Anthropic path moves from three
system marks plus the newest user block to stable, previous tail and tail, the
interactive hour riding the stable breakpoint; `AnthropicOptions.cache` and the
`is_environment` content sniff in both adapters are deleted; the Responses wire
renders the block separator as a paragraph break in `input` and `instructions`

## Why

#743: after D291 the marks were still three sites, a bool and a sniff on
`<environment>`, so nothing stopped a request carrying no mark or a mark on the
block that changes every request, which wrote an entry no later request could read
at 1.25x on the whole prompt (10 of 10 zero reads in the 2026-09-28 probes); the
owner: "This class of cache failure must be prevented from being representable" and
"always send cache markers"; the probes also showed a previous-tail breakpoint is a
free read that covers Bedrock's raw-block lookback (3 of 88 Opus transitions
appended past 20 blocks), a one-shot tail has reuse near zero against a 0.28 break-
even, Gemini re-snapshots the prompt at its last breakpoint, and the Responses
adapter sent the raw `\u{1d}` to api.openai.com and codex; the first cut was named
`CachePlan` with `Purpose`, `Anchor` and `Route`, and the owner rejected the first
for colliding with the plan primitive ("could not tell what it was"), while the
other three collided with `plan::capacity::Purpose`, the hashline `Anchor` and
`ext::orchestrate::Route`. Ceilings: the `Automatic` dialect spells nothing, since
no explicit breakpoint is known to be accepted by direct api.openai.com or the
Responses API; a reply the transform drops (an errored turn) hides that request's
previous tail from the next request, costing one free read; the previous tail is
found by structure, not a ledger; the TTL is fixed at write time until the adaptive
rule lands

## Reversible via

Restore `AnthropicOptions.cache`, the per-block system marks and `is_environment` in
`anthropic.rs`, `mark_cache_breakpoints` in `openai.rs`, fold `transient` back into
`messages` in `loop/src/run.rs`, and drop `crates/ai/src/breakpoints.rs`;
`LlmContext.transient` and `schema` stay, additive
