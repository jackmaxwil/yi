# D381: `spend.dayAlertUsd` shows one `spend_alert` notice per multiple of the local...

Status: accepted

## Decision

`spend.dayAlertUsd` shows one `spend_alert` notice per multiple of the local day's
billed dollars crossed across every session, seeded from today's session files at
each alarm's first attach and kept across its re-attaches, grown by the live
session's replies and its children, and restarted from zero past local midnight

## Why

The per-session `spend.alertUsd` cannot see four sessions that each stay under it,
and the owner chose "Warn only, per session and per day"

## Reversible via

Drop the `dayAlertUsd` key and `SpendAlarm::day_usd`
