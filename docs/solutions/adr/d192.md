# D192: the third reasoning cut of a prompt clamps thinking off

Status: accepted

## Decision

The third reasoning cut of a prompt clamps thinking off: `current_effort` drops to
the model's lowest supported rung for the rest of the run and the redrive says so
(`CUT_CLAMP_REDRIVE_TEXT`), where rungs two through five repeated `CUT_REDRIVE_TEXT`
verbatim; `CUT_STOP_AT` stays six as the backstop for a model with no off

## Why

Music-harmony and coq-block-bound each hit the output limit six times and stopped,
coq without writing a line, for ~75k tokens of derivation (the 2026-09-11 v4 sweep):
the nudge asked for the write but left the model the budget to keep deriving, and a
cut cannot recur once there is no thinking to cut

## Reversible via

Restore the flat ladder; `CUT_CLAMP_AT` is one constant and the clamp one line
