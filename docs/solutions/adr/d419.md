# D419: no CI job plays the TUI scenes

Status: accepted

## Decision

No CI job plays the TUI scenes: `.forgejo/workflows/tui.yml` is deleted, with
`tui_see.py`'s `ci`, `post` and `publish` verbs and their comment, upload and live-
gallery release code, and the review bot no longer waits for or reads a TUI snapshot
comment (supersedes the PR-comment decision of docs/changes/2026-10-11-tui-see-pr-
comment.md and the live gallery of 2026-10-12-live-tui-gallery.md); `tui-see run`,
`diff`, `gallery`, `watch`, `harvest` and `monkey` stay as local tools, `--host
term` included

## Why

The owner: "Remove the Yi TUI screenshot job completely. it provides little value
for the cost"; on 2026-10-12 a TUI pull request's build, shots and comment jobs
queued behind the shared runners for up to an hour, and the review round waited up
to 30 minutes for that comment before reading

## Reversible via

Restore tui.yml and the `ci`, `compose`, `post`, `upload` and `publish` paths in
scripts/tui_see.py from this commit's parent, and `snapshots`, `settled_notes` and
`forge_pr.run_status` in the review
