# D602: Double Ctrl+C in the console detaches and leaves `yi serve` running (amends...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Double Ctrl+C in the console detaches and leaves `yi serve` running (amends D356's
parenthetical "ctrl+c twice in the console" and the console quit note of
#1032/#1350); stopping the daemon stays on the command palette's `stop the daemon
and quit` (`_yi/shutdown`)

## Why

A double Ctrl+C meant to leave a pane killed the shared daemon and every worker and
background session on the machine

## Reversible via

Restore `self.stop_daemon_and_quit(outbound)` in `crates/console/src/app.rs` on the
second Ctrl+C press
