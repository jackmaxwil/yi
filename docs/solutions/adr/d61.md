# D61: an edit renders its diff in Normal mode, and diff rows wrap

Status: accepted

## Decision

An edit or write renders its diff in **Normal** mode rather than behind Verbose,
and the transcript's diff rows wrap by column rather than truncate. U15's
head-plus-digest contract still stands for every other tool: the diff body is the
exception, granted to the calls whose entire subject is a change. The flood risk
is answered by a budget — 8 hunks and 40 rows in Normal — that keeps change rows,
trims edge context, drops whole hunks from the tail and names on screen what it
dropped. Verbose renders the diff whole.

## Rationale

The call whose whole subject is a change was the one cell that showed none of it.
`updated; first change at line 2` names a location and withholds the content,
which is the inverse of what a reader wants from an edit: they know where, they
came for what. Recovering the content meant knowing Ctrl+O existed and then
re-reading the cell in the other mode — a two-step detour for the single most
common thing the agent does to a repository.

Truncation is right in U12's approval box and wrong in the transcript, and the
difference is height. The approval box has a fixed height and must not jitter, so
a long row is cut. The transcript has no height budget at all, and a truncated
diff row drops the column that marks an addition — the reader loses not detail
but polarity, which is the one thing the row exists to convey. Word-wrap fails
for the neighbouring reason: it loses the column alignment that makes two
versions comparable. Wrapping by column with a blank gutter that keeps the sign
column preserves both.

Hiding the body was never a real answer to the flood risk either. A budget bounds
the cost and says so, so a reader knows a hunk was dropped and can open Verbose;
mode-gating bounds nothing and tells no one. Ranking change rows above context,
trimming edge context to one row and dropping from the tail keeps the rows that
carry the change when the budget bites.

The renderer takes codex's four-layer row and its palette values verbatim, forked
on `ColorTier` and dark/light, because those values were tuned against real
terminals. `edit`'s old numbered-line body is deleted rather than kept beside the
new one: it rendered the same information worse, and two bodies for one call is
the duplication the guardrails exist to prevent.

## Reversal path

Render an empty `Vec` from `yi-tui::diffview::render`; the digest line and the
typed body are untouched behind it.
