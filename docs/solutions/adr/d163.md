# D163: a request is cut at a reasoning budget and re-driven, and no turn is forced...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A request is cut at a reasoning budget and re-driven, and no turn is forced to a
tool: the loop counts the chars of every reasoning delta until text or a tool call
starts, and at `REASONING_CHAR_CAP` (48,000) sets the interrupt signal's cut flag,
which the three provider pumps read between SSE events and stop on (dropping the
reader closes the connection); the cut turn is kept as a bare length stop with no
thinking block and `usage.reasoning` at chars/4, so the runaway never rides a later
request as cached input, and the D153 ladder re-drives it with `{rung, cut: true,
reasoningChars}` and the text "The reply hit the output limit before any tool call.
Pick the most boring viable option and act on it now: make the tool call, then
explain."; `length_force` and the forced `bash` tool_choice are deleted;
`extract.py` counts `spiral_cut`

## Why

Calibrated on the 158 sessions of rows 0018-0023: 1,633 healthy thinking blocks (p99
23k chars, seven over 48k) against 29 spirals (min 72k chars); omp's near-duplicate
(trigram-Jaccard 0.8 over 16 segments) and lexical-stall (novelty 0.2 for 8
segments) rules, ported and replayed, tripped 0 of 29 spirals and 3 healthy blocks,
and no novelty or similarity threshold separated the two sets, so length is the only
deterministic separator this corpus offers; the forced bash of D153 produced empty
`write {}` calls on every photonic attempt of row 0023 (docs/plans/2026-09-08-pass-
levers.md S7)

## Reversible via

Delete `reasoning.rs`, the cut flag and the three pump checks; the ladder returns to
D153 without its forced tool
