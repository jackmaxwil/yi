# D155: a todo item has an id and a label matches by unique prefix

Status: accepted

## Decision

A todo item has an id and a label matches by unique prefix: every item carries
`t<n>`, minted per session and never reused, rendered in every listing and `next:`
line, accepted by every op beside `label`; a label resolves exact, then by id, then
by a unique case-insensitive prefix of eight characters or more with backticks
stripped, and an ambiguous prefix is refused naming the candidates; a label over
eighty characters is cut into its note, never refused, and a cut label shows `…`; a
call with no `op` whose fields name one op (`list` is set, `items` is append,
`label` with `evidence` is done, with `reason` is drop, with `on` is block) takes it
and says so

## Why

Row 0021 spent four to six turns per attempt on `no todo labelled`, `exceeds 80
chars` and `op is required`, and the D148 seeding cut bun's decisive clause mid-word
at eighty characters so all three attempts steered by the truncated label (issue
#279)

## Reversible via

Drop the `id` field (additive, old sessions rehydrate without it), the prefix step
and the inference
