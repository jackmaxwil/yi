# D330: `rlm.run(role="worker")` is a narrow child that changes files, amending D307...

Status: accepted

## Decision

`rlm.run(role="worker")` is a narrow child that changes files, amending D307 and
D308: its system prompt is `prompts/worker.md`, the permission mode, the project's
instruction files (AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md, followed only when their fence says
`trust="granted"`, as the root does) and the language packs (`ext::narrow`), with no
identity, doctrine, skill catalog or orchestration; `tools` from `read`, `grep`,
`edit`, `write`, `bash` and `get_context` (the first four by default); `turns` 12,
at most 40, the last refusing tool calls; its partition and schema are inlined as a
reader's; its writes and commands pass its wall, the permission broker and the
user's gate rules as a root child's do, the user's reminder rules reach it, and it
compacts on its own model and re-arms its reminders after; it stands inside the
worker cap, may take `isolation`, and refuses `fork` and `check`; a plan delegation
still spawns `role="root"`

## Why

The owner chose "Worker role brings them (Recommended)" for a child that edits code
on 2026-09-28; a child that had to write took the root's whole prompt and every
tool, about 20k tokens before its task (#931)

## Reversible via

Drop the `worker` arm of `reader::parse`; `role="root"` is unchanged
