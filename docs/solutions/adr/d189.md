# D189: the re-drive of a prompt's second and later cuts quotes where the cut stopped

Status: accepted

## Decision

The re-drive of a prompt's second and later cuts quotes where the cut stopped:
`CUT_REDRIVE_TEXT` is followed by one sentence saying the cut reasoning is not in
the conversation and the last 8,000 chars of the cut turn's thinking
(`CUT_QUOTE_CHARS`) inside `<cut_reasoning>`, read from the settled turn before
`cut_message` strips it; a prompt's first cut, the effort, the tool choice and
`CUT_STOP_AT` are unchanged, and a follow-up prompt counts its cuts from one again,
so its first cut quotes nothing; on `openrouter/z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` each request
after a prompt's second to fifth cut ends in a user message 8,173 chars longer and
every request still sends `reasoning: {"effort": "low"}` (amends D163 and D178)

## Why

A cut turn reached the next request empty (its thinking stripped, and an assistant
message with no text or call is not sent), so each re-drive asked the model to act
on a derivation it could no longer see: on the 2026-09-11 v4 sweep coq-block-bound's
six cut requests grew from 11,148 to 11,380 input tokens, the re-drives' words
alone, and none called a tool; music-harmony was cut five times before its one write
and freecad-spring-clip four; of the sweep's 11 first-cut re-drives that got a next
turn 7 called a tool on it, of the 12 later ones 3; 8,000 chars hold nine in ten of
the sweep's 1,455 thinking blocks that ended in a tool call (p90 7,594);
glm-5.3-flash already sends its lowest effort and its route refuses `effort: none`
with HTTP 400 (the 2026-09-10 probe), so the request's words were the lever left

## Reversible via

Drop the quote arm of `length_redrive` and `reasoning::tail`; the cut carries its
char count alone again
