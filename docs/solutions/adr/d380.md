# D380: `yi stats --by prices` is a view that least-squares fits input, output,...

Status: accepted

## Decision

`yi stats --by prices` is a view that least-squares fits input, output, cacheRead
and cacheWrite prices per (model, upstream) route from billed totals, needs 30
replies, shows its residual and flags a component more than 20% from the catalog;
nothing reads it back, so `other_families` and `find_models` still sort by catalog
price

## Why

The catalog priced GLM 5.3 at 0.039 in against about 0.90 billed, but wiring the fit
into the jury needs the sessions directory and a scan inside the runtime, which
costs more than the view until a decision needs it; tiers are not fitted, so a long-
context route shows a residual

## Reversible via

Drop the `prices` view and `Observed`
