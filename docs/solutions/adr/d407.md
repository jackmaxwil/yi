# D407: settings is a console pane with a live Session tab over the session's ACP...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Settings is a console pane with a live Session tab over the session's ACP options, a
tab per group of every config.json leaf, and a Keys tab; the keys overlay is deleted
into that tab

## Why

The owner asked for "a new settings page inspired by Omp" opened from a gear in the
bottom-left, and chose "As a pane", "Both, in two sections", "Everything in config,
organized into subpages/tabs" and "Yes, as a Keys tab"

## Reversible via

Delete PaneContent::Settings and restore Mode::Keys with palette::render_keys
