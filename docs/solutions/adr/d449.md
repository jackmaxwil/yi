# D449: A five-field cron fires on a day matching day-of-month or day-of-week when...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A five-field cron fires on a day matching day-of-month or day-of-week when both
fields are restricted (amends D283's local-clock cron with crontab(5) semantics)

## Why

30 4 1,15 * 5 fired only on a 1st or 15th that was also a Friday

## Reversible via

Restore the AND in matches_cron_fields
