# D518: the classifier runs on TypeSafe's Jev (`typesafe/jev-1.13`) at OpenRouter's...

Status: accepted

## Decision

The classifier runs on TypeSafe's Jev (`typesafe/jev-1.13`) at OpenRouter's
`/api/v1/systemone`, keyed by the OpenRouter key, and the Laya sidecar Yi started,
installed and probed is deleted; with the classifier on, typed messages and judged
commands go to TypeSafe through OpenRouter; approval asks the V2 question (task,
last two answered calls, project root, writable roots, protected branches,
checkpoint note) and allows at P(safe) 0.8, 0.9 for a destructive command (revises
D297, D301, D356, D364, D497)

## Why

Measured on 160 commands (yi-ergo 2026-10-29-jev): Laya allowed dangerous commands
at every cut point under every question, Jev with the V2 question allowed none at
0.8 (53 of 80 ordinary) or 0.9 (27 of 80), at about $0.00002 a call

## Reversible via

Unset `models.classifier`; the old sidecar comes back from git with its D-rows
