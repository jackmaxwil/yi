# D179: the repeat breaker counts a batch over six turns and sees a text-only turn

Status: accepted

## Decision

The repeat breaker counts a batch over six turns and sees a text-only turn: each
turn's tool batch, or with no calls its text collapsed to single spaces and cut at
256 characters, joins a window of the last six signatures; the third copy in the
window sends D152's hidden `repeat_break` once, until a batch new to the window
starts another stretch, and the sixth, which must fill the window, ends the run, so
two batches taking turns are steered once and never ended; a follow-up prompt starts
an empty window, as it starts its own cut count; a bash job poll and a cut turn,
which keeps no text, still yield nothing and stay out of the window; the message
drops "with the same result", since results were never compared (amends D152)

## Why

D152 compared a turn only with the one before it and gave a tool-less turn no
signature, so two batches taking turns, a call repeated between job polls and a
reply repeated to a re-driving intercept never counted: over 1,044 turns in thirteen
audit-slice trials 147 batches repeated, four of them back to back, and
`repeat_break` fired once, while cargo Lb45bGG spent 273 of its 444 turns, about
$2.05, answering one intercept (the 2026-09-10 harness audit, B5 and G6); two
batches taking turns hold the count at three, so a steer on every such turn would
itself keep two replies re-driving without end; replayed over 253 archived sessions
(4,148 turns) the rule sends 31 steers in 15 sessions where D152 sent 6 in 3, and
ends Lb45bGG at turn 215; 16 of the 31 follow an earlier steer in the same session,
since a batch new to the window re-arms it, 13 of them on a check re-run between
todo calls whose evidence changed each time; four land on an edit and a check taking
turns, two of them in v4 trials, and in such a loop each fresh edit re-arms the
steer, so every check from the third on draws it

## Reversible via

Restore the comparison with the previous turn only, `None` for a tool-less turn, the
steer at the third identical batch in a row and the old message
