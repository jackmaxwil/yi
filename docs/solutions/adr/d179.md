# D179: the repeat breaker counts a batch over six turns and sees a text-only turn

Status: accepted

## Decision

The repeat breaker counts a batch over six turns and sees a text-only turn: each
turn's tool batch, or with no calls its text collapsed to single spaces and cut at
256 characters, joins a window of the last six signatures; a third or later copy in
the window sends D152's hidden `repeat_break` once the last three signed turns each
repeated a batch already in the window, so a check re-run after each fresh edit
never draws it and an identical batch draws it at its fourth run, and it goes once
until a batch new to the window starts another stretch; the sixth copy, which must
fill the window, ends the run, so two batches taking turns are steered once and
never ended; a follow-up prompt starts an empty window, as it starts its own cut
count; a bash job poll and a cut turn, which keeps no text, still yield nothing and
stay out of the window; the message drops "with the same result", since results were
never compared (amends D152)

## Why

D152 compared a turn only with the one before it and gave a tool-less turn no
signature, so two batches taking turns, a call repeated between job polls and a
reply repeated to a re-driving intercept never counted: over 1,044 turns in thirteen
audit-slice trials 147 batches repeated, four of them back to back, and
`repeat_break` fired once, while cargo Lb45bGG spent 273 of its 444 turns, about
$2.05, answering one intercept (the 2026-09-10 harness audit, B5 and G6); two
batches taking turns hold the count at three, so a steer on every such turn would
itself keep two replies re-driving without end; replayed over 253 archived sessions
(4,148 turns), a steer at any third copy sent 31 in 15 sessions where D152 sent 6 in
3, and four landed on an edit and a check taking turns, two of them in v4 trials,
where each fresh edit re-armed the steer so every check from the third on drew it;
with three repeating turns required the rule sends 11 steers in 4 sessions, none
with an edit in the five turns before: nine in three search-loop fixture rollouts
that kept re-running one passing check and re-sending its todo call, six of those
after an earlier steer in the same rollout, and two in Lb45bGG, which it still ends
at turn 215

## Reversible via

Restore the comparison with the previous turn only, `None` for a tool-less turn, the
steer at the third identical batch in a row and the old message; or steer at any
third copy in the window, without the three repeating turns
