# D231: one `rlm` surface, and every call on it works with or without `await`

Status: accepted

## Decision

One `rlm` surface, and every call on it works with or without `await`: the kernel
preamble binds `rlm` to the `rlm` module itself and `fetch` and `bash` to its
functions, so `_RLMCallable` and the placeholder's own list of names are gone;
`rlm/__init__.py::_public` puts a function in `rlm.__all__`, which `help(rlm)` and
the ready check read, and wraps an `async def` in `_either_way`, as the handle
methods (`RLMSpawnHandle.result` and `send`, `BashHandle.tail`, `poll`, `kill` and
`wait`) are wrapped: a call whose site takes the coroutine (the operand of `await`,
a `return`, a `lambda` body, a `yield`, an argument, by name, of an asyncio call
that takes one such as `gather`, `ensure_future`, `create_task` or `wait_for`, or
the receiver of a coroutine method such as `.__await__`) gets the coroutine, and any
other call runs to completion on the kernel's loop, re-entered through
`nest_asyncio`, and returns its value; the site is the caller's `ast.Call` found at
the CALL instruction's source position, and a site with no readable source keeps the
coroutine, as before

## Why

6 of 8 dogfood trials and 26 corpus calls in 22 sessions called `rlm` without
`await`; a coroutine nobody awaits starts nothing and raises nowhere, so `rlm.send`
never sent and `rlm.status()` printed a coroutine, and the hint came after the fact;
the host's reply needs the loop the cell holds, but `nest_asyncio` already makes
that loop re-entrant; a call that always ran at once would run `asyncio.gather` one
call at a time and block the plan library's `ensure_future(rlm.wait())`, and a proxy
that ran on first use would fail `json.dumps` and `isinstance`, so the syntax at the
call site is the one signal both readings agree on, and every corpus miss (a `print`
argument, a bare statement, an assignment, a loop's iterable) is a value use; the
preloaded object was a second hand-kept list that had already lost
`result(timeout=)`, and `__all__` a third that left out `status`, `service`, `put`,
`get`, `ls` and `list_agents`

## Reversible via

Drop `_either_way` from `_public` and the handle methods, and every call is a
coroutine again; `__all__` stays generated and the preamble keeps the module
