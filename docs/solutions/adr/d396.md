# D396: a drive scene containing `tick <ms>` runs on a frozen `Clock` that every...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A drive scene containing `tick <ms>` runs on a frozen `Clock` that every on-screen
duration and animation reads; `tick` advances it at most 16 ms per step and draws,
`snap-strip <name> <n> <ms>` parses into snaps and ticks, `hold <event type> [n]`
stops the faux stream before the n-th event of that wire type until `release`, which
fails the drive when the hold was never reached and waits (on real time, at most 5
s, red past it) until the released message's end has arrived before the next step,
so a frozen reveal never depends on how much landed in real time; a child's stream
does not count toward the root's hold; deadlines, polls and input windows stay on
real time, and a solo scene with no seed starts from an empty session under a fixed
id

## Why

The owner asked to see streaming and animation ("Yes, whatever it costs"), and faux
streams instantly, so no spinner, reveal or running turn ever reached a frame; two
runs of a held, ticked scene now produce identical snaps

## Reversible via

Drop `Clock` back to `Instant::now()` at each site, and the four drive steps with
`FauxHold`
