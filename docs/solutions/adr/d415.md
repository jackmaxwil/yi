# D415: solo commits rows the viewport's top already shows by adopting them where...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Solo commits rows the viewport's top already shows by adopting them where they
stand, and a short screen whose viewport leaves fewer than two rows above it commits
through a full-screen scroll and a repaint, never a one-row scroll region

## Why

A committed paragraph was written above the region again while it stayed on screen,
so history jumped by its height and the shrunk region floated the composer up (4 to
16 rows a frame, the floor moving 27 times in one reply), and on a short screen a
one-row region, which terminals ignore, scrolled the whole screen and wrote composer
borders into scrollback (5 rows corrupt and 7 lines lost at 12x80)

## Reversible via

Drop `Terminal::adopt` and its call in `draw_frame`, and restore the full-screen
branch of `insert_before`
