# D487: a plan child's ending reaches the owner as one line naming its product's...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A plan child's ending reaches the owner as one line naming its product's address
(plan:// on accept, history://<child> otherwise) and no reap block, revising the
promotion invariant at mailbox.rs:883-884 and D230

## Why

Twelve plan children cost about 24 KB on every later request, and reclaim never cuts
the block (reclaim.rs:320-326)

## Reversible via

Restore the clamped body in SubagentHost::reap
