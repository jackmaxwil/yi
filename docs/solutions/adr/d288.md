# D288: a PR opens as a draft against a filled template

Status: accepted

## Decision

A PR opens as a draft against a filled template: `just pr open` titles it `WIP:
<subject>`, Forgejo's own draft marker, which the forge refuses to merge, and the
title rule (`check_pr_metadata.title_errors`) judges the subject after exactly that
prefix as the merge will carry it, while a commit subject and every other scratch
form (`WIP`, `WIP:x`, `[WIP]`, `fixup!`) stay refused; `just pr ready N` takes the
marker off and `just land` readies its own PR; the body owes every section of
`.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md` (`REQUIRED`, pinned to the template's headings by
the selfcheck), each present, non-empty and holding no `<!--`, judged by the `title`
job and by `just pr open` before it creates anything; the template gains "Why
needed", "Deleted / alternatives" (absorbing "LOC and justification"), "Risk and
rollback" and "Performance", and "Screenshots" folds into "UI changes"

## Why

30 of the 80 PR bodies merged from #395 to #597 kept a template placeholder, every
heading present, and the one structural check passed them; the last 80 merged PRs
had one formal review and a 0.66 h median open-to-merge, so a PR needs a state that
says it has not been reviewed yet (plan `docs/plans/2026-09-26-pr-lifecycle.md`,
stage 0 of the PR lifecycle; the review rounds that will ready a draft are its
stages 1 and 3)

## Reversible via

Revert `title_errors` to `subject_errors`, drop `template_problems` from the two
callers, and `just pr open` stops prefixing
