# D566: every child is sent a pointer in place of a skill catalog, `Skills

Status: accepted

## Decision

Every child is sent a pointer in place of a skill catalog, `Skills:
read("yi://skills") lists them; read("yi://skills/<name>") loads one.`, and a second
line naming each skill its parent read through `yi://skills/<name>` (amends D334,
whose narrow child carried no catalog, and D290, whose full child listed
`~/.yi/skills` and the repository's skills); `read("yi://skills")` serves every
listed skill with its whole description, fitted to 32 KiB with a cut row naming kept
of total, the cap and the per-skill read; a reader with no `read` is sent no pointer

## Why

The owner, 2026-11-05: "narrow children should get skill catalog on demand. should
get a pointer to skill catalog"; a worker could not look up a skill, and
`read("yi://skills")` was refused as a bad address

## Reversible via

Drop the `Pointer` arm of `ProjectResources` and the `skills` arm of `resolve_docs`
