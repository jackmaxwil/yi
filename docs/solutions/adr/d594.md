# D594: Esc and Ctrl-C close a completion list and keep the draft, superseding...

Status: accepted

## Decision

Esc and Ctrl-C close a completion list and keep the draft, superseding #2149's "Esc
and Ctrl-C close the list and empty the box, as before"

## Why

With the draft always in the box, keeping it is the one rule a typed list and a Tab-
opened list share, where #2149 emptied the one and kept the other; coordinator's
call for this PR, 2026-11-12

## Reversible via

Make `Composer::dismiss_assist` clear the text when a typed list closes
