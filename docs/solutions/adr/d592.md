# D592: The chat composer keeps its draft and draws assistance over it

Status: accepted

## Decision

The chat composer keeps its draft and draws assistance over it: a curly underline
under a misspelled prose word, a gray ghost suffix with the cursor at the line's
end, and lists for `/` verbs, `$` skills, `@` files, fetch schemes and emoji above
the box. A list opens only on a typed edit (a paste or a recalled history entry
opens none), Tab takes what is drawn and otherwise completes the token at the
cursor, Enter on a `/` row runs it, Esc closes the list and keeps the draft, and an
`ask_user` card that arrives takes the list's place at once; a replacement (a row,
an emoji, a correction) is one ctrl+z that puts back what was typed, and each list
that opens walks the folder and the skills again; the `/` and `@` popups and #2149's
command-list popup go, and skills list under `$` only, so no skill can shadow a verb

## Why

The bottom stack was a composer or a view that replaced it, so a list hid the draft
and a paste landed in the hidden box; one list over the draft serves every token
kind

## Reversible via

Restore `Bottom::Command` and `Bottom::File` in crates/tui/src/app.rs and their key
handling in input.rs
