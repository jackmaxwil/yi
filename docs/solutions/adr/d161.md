# D161: a bash result is compressed before its middle is cut, and the cut starts at...

Status: accepted

## Decision

A bash result is compressed before its middle is cut, and the cut starts at 8 KiB:
`REDUCE_FLOOR` is 8,192 bytes (the bash description names the same number and a test
ties them); past it `compress` keeps only the text after the last carriage return
per line, trims trailing whitespace and collapses blank runs, counts a line repeated
anywhere later on its first occurrence as `[×N]`, and keeps only the last instance
of a progress line (`42%`, `[===>`, `━━`); only then does `cap_lines` omit a middle,
taking head and tail by the larger of the line cap and what fits in two thirds and
one third of the floor, with the `[N lines omitted: A-B]` marker and the `[full
output: path]` pointer unchanged; `extract.py` counts `reduced_results`

## Why

Across the 158 sessions of rows 0018-0023 the reducer emitted 77 pointers and no
rollout read one back, only 3 results exceeded 8 KiB after reduction, and heat-
pump's decisive checkbox rows sat in the second half of a 700-line dump the 120-line
cap omitted (issue #291); a floor that most outputs clear and a compression that
spends the budget on distinct lines put the decisive rows in the result instead of
behind a pointer (docs/archive/plans/2026-09-08-pass-levers.md S3)

## Reversible via

`REDUCE_FLOOR` back to 2,048, delete `compress` and `is_progress`, and `cap_lines`
loses its byte budget
