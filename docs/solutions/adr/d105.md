# D105: the plan frontmatter is JSON, and the YAML subset codec is gone

Status: accepted (supersedes the frontmatter clause of D97)

## Decision

The plan file stays a Markdown document between two `---` lines, and what sits
between them is JSON, written by `serde_json::to_string_pretty` and read by
`serde_json::from_str`. `PlanStore::render` and `store::parse_document` are the
only two seams a plan document passes through, and both are serde_json calls.
The hand-rolled YAML subset codec (`crates/runtime/src/plan/yaml.rs`, 970 lines
including its tests) is deleted, along with the write-time emit, re-parse and
compare guard it needed.

## Why

D97 chose YAML for the human with an editor and hand-rolled it because `toml`
is banned by name and the transitive budget had one unit left. Both halves were
re-measured on the review of the landing.

Every YAML crate in the ecosystem carries `unsafe` in its tree: `serde_yaml`
and `serde_yaml_ng` sit on `unsafe-libyaml`, a c2rust transliteration in which
every function is unsafe; `yaml-rust2`, `saphyr` and `serde-saphyr` are
themselves unsafe-free but sit on `arraydeque` (72 unsafe sites) and
`hashlink` (81). Each costs between two and nine transitive crates against a
budget of one. A hand-rolled parser behind a user-editable file is 700 lines of
attack surface this tree would own forever. serde_json is already the parser
behind every other durable shape here, is vetted, and is fuzzed upstream.

The cost is the human's: no comments in the frontmatter, quotes and commas.
The body under the frontmatter is still Markdown, and `## <label>` sections
still carry a todo's prose. Nothing shipped with YAML frontmatter before this
row, so there is no migration.

## Reversible via

Reinstate a codec behind `store::parse_document` and `PlanStore::render`; every
reader and writer already goes through those two seams, and the round-trip
guard in `PlanStore::write` comes back with it.
