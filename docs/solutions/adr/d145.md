# D145: a delta is a delta

Status: accepted

## Decision

A delta is a delta: an `AssistantMessageEvent` carries its own bytes and nothing
else — `Start`, `Done` and `Error` carry a whole message, every `*Start`, `*Delta`
and `*End` carries an index and its delta or settled block —
`AgentEvent::MessageUpdate` carries only the event, and one accumulator,
`yi_types::event::apply`, folds the stream into the message in place for the loop,
the TUI's reveal and a child's cell; the providers keep their own accumulator for
`Done` and clone it once

## Why

On Terminal-Bench v4 `photonic-waveguide-routing` a 131,072-token turn streamed
130,496 `message_update` events, each a snapshot of the whole partial message and
each carrying it twice (`message` and the delta's `partial`), 43.8 GB into a harness
that buffers stdout, and the OS killed the run (ledger 0017, issue #254); the wire
was O(n²) in the answer and nothing on it read the snapshot — the CLI and ACP read
deltas, harbor and the extractor read `message_end`, and the TUI's reveal can fold
deltas itself; a 100 KB faux echo streams 409 KB before and 309 KB after at 63
deltas (the rest is the prompt and the three whole-message events), a number that no
longer grows with the delta count, and 20,000 one-byte deltas serialize under 1.3 MB
where they were 200 MB

## Reversible via

Restore `partial` on the delta variants and `message` on `MessageUpdate`, and have
the loop clone the accumulator into each; Pi's wire is what that was
