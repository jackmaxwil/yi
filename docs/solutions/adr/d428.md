# D428: The Tape pane leaves, reversing D267

Status: accepted

## Decision

The Tape pane leaves, reversing D267: the pane, its ⌘Y and ⌥y keys, the console's
`_yi/tape` request, the worker's `_yi/tape` route and the `tape::tape` fold go, and
⌘Y opens the terminal pane; the `yi_types::tape` shapes stay, since a wire shape and
its fixture are never deleted

the terminal pane, and the owner chose "Tape pane (⌘Y) (Recommended)"; rewinding to
a turn, the Tape's one action, stays on the message menu's "Rewind to here"
