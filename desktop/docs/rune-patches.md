# Rune patches

What the app needs from the fork (`https://github.com/jackmaxwil/rune`, branch `yi-desktop`, pinned in `docs/deps.md`) that Rune does not do yet. One row a patch: the bug a person sees, the smallest change, and what the app does until the patch lands. A patch made in the fork is one commit and this row names it.

| Patch | What a person sees | Where | The change | Until then, in the app |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A system material ignores the page zoom (fork `d047dfd4`) | Under `Cx::zoom(1.25)` a root carrying `SystemMaterial::Glass` gets a native view 1/1.25 of the window (608 by 448 of 760 by 560): the rest of a window with no opaque layer of its own is clear, a click there goes to the window behind, and the window looks like a pop-up. Seen in Settings, Themes and every pulled-out pane (DF22). | `crates/rune/src/window/draw.rs:427` | `l.native.materials.present(&rt.materials, rt.window_scale(), ..)` in place of `rt.env.scale`: `MaterialDraw.rect` is in device pixels and `MaterialDraw::points` must divide by the window's scale, not the window's scale times the zoom. | Every window draws the shell's wash and tint under what it shows (`shell::view::backdrop`), so no pixel is clear. A theme whose `wash` is 0 leaves the tint alone (20% by default) over the part the glass misses. |
| A second stroke on the title bar zooms the window (fork `826baf80`) | Moving the window in two quick strokes (grab, drag, let go, grab again within the double-click interval) fills the screen instead of moving it: the window followed the pointer, so the second press lands on the same window point, and the titlebar pairs presses by window point and time alone. Seen on the top bar at every zoom; a pause of more than half a second between strokes moved it. | `crates/rune/src/runtime/input/titlebar.rs` (`step`), `crates/rune/src/runtime/layout_read.rs:504` (`set_window_origin`) | A new `Event::Moved` that drops the pending pair (`keep_secondary(state, None)`), stepped from `set_window_origin` whenever the window's origin changes, which the host already calls on every `WindowEvent::Moved`; `set_window_origin` becomes `pub` so a test can say the window moved. Test: `pointer_hit::a_press_after_dragging_the_window_drags_again_and_a_still_double_press_zooms`. | Nothing in the app can break the pair. Once the patch lands, `shell::top_bar::tests` (DF23's report) defends it through `root::view` at zooms 0.5 to 2.5. |
| The Window menu lists no windows (fork `d047dfd4`) | macOS lists an app's windows at the foot of its Window menu only when `NSApp.windowsMenu` is set; Rune never sets it, so Settings, Themes and pulled-out panes cannot be picked there. | `crates/rune/src/native_menu/default_macos.rs`, `add_top` (line 252) | When `name == "Window"`, after `insertItem_atIndex`: `NSApplication::sharedApplication(mtm).setWindowsMenu(Some(&menu))`. AppKit then adds a row for each titled window and keeps it current. | Each pane's window is titled after its pane (Settings, Themes, Files), so the list is right once the patch lands; ⌘` (`window.cycle`, Window menu "Cycle Through Windows") brings the next window forward. |
| A resting material halves the frame rate (fork `1fab9bb1`) | With `SystemMaterial::Glass` on the root, the window drew 68 to 88 frames a second on a 120 Hz display with a quarter to a half of them late, while each frame took under 3 ms to build: the wait for a drawable was 12 to 17 ms at the 95th percentile. Scrolling, a streaming answer and every animation stuttered. `RUNE_SYSTEM_MATERIAL=unsynced` held 116 to 119 with the same picture (`just feel-live --material unsynced`). | `crates/rune/src/system_material/macos.rs` (`Materials::update`, `present`), `mod.rs` (`SyncGate`) | While a native material is up the Metal layer presented every drawable inside a Core Animation transaction, so the material and the content land together. A material that does not move gains nothing from it. `update` now takes the frame's draws and syncs only while a view's frame, radius or alpha changes and for eight frames after; at rest the layer presents plainly, and `present` opens no transaction. | Nothing: the app's one material covers the window and moves only with it, which a live resize already syncs. |
