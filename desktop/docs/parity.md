# Parity with the mockup

One row for every mockup frame the fixtures were read from (`fixtures/mockup/panes/*.text.json`), and one for the whole first frame. The table is written by a script from what `just scenes` rendered, so it cannot drift from the scenes:

```
just scenes
python3 tools/parity/report.py --write docs/parity.md --pairs ~/Development/t3-rune-port/plan/reports/parity
```

- **Text** is the share of the frame's on-screen strings the scene draws, after the exceptions in `fixtures/mockup/intended.json` are taken out; **Before exceptions** is the same share with none taken out. A scene passes at 0.97.
- **Sizes in** counts the measured sizes within one point of the mockup's, before exceptions.
- **Exceptions** are that frame's entries of `intended.json`, each with its reason. The whole window also takes the word exceptions of the panes read from frame 1.
- **What a person sees differ** is written by hand in `tools/parity/seen.json`, from looking at each scene beside its still. The pairs are saved as `<frame>.png`, scene left, still right.
