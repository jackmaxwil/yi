Root cause
----------

The preliminary finding of operator error is withdrawn. The root cause was a watchdog
fault in outstation firmware 4.1.7, which froze the outputs so that the operator's
commands had no effect.

Recommendations
---------------

1. Update the outstation firmware at Eastmere Intake to 4.2.1.

2. Add an output-frozen alarm to the control room display.
