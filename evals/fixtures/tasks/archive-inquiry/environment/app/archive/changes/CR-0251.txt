Description
-----------

Petra Vosk confirmed that spares for the surge vessel are held at the central store
and that the reorder level is 23 units. Members of the networks team asked for the
seasonal demand figures to be circulated before the next meeting.

to cover additional painting. It was withdrawn before decision and has no effect.

allow.
