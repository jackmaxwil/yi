Quillfen Water Cooperative
MINUTES OF THE BOARD, meeting BM-2031-07
Held: 2031-07-18
Present: Hollis Wray, Lucan Marsh, Oona Farrow, Ysolde Quint, Bram Ketteridge, Tamsin Orrel
Apologies: Oona Farrow

1. Minutes of the previous meeting
----------------------------------

The minutes of the previous meeting were agreed as a true record, subject to any
correction recorded below.

2. Programme ownership
----------------------

The telemetry link to Osier Bank dropped twice in the period; both outages were under
31 minutes and no alarms were missed. Ansel Brightwater noted that the calibration
certificate for the level sensor expires next quarter and has booked the visit.

The chair recorded that ownership of the telemetry renewal programme passes from Petra
Vosk to Oona Farrow from 1 August.

3. Operations report
--------------------

Tamsin Orrel presented a short paper on sensor drift and recommended a comparison
check at Osier Bank every 34 weeks. Hollis Wray reported that the wet well ladder at
Merrow Basin had been inspected and found serviceable, with minor wear noted on the
seals. An energy audit of Merrow Basin suggested that running the penstock actuator
off-peak would reduce cost without affecting service. The stores count showed 30
discrepancies, all traced to items issued to vans without a ticket. A walkdown of
Tolland Sluice found the signage faded; replacement boards have been ordered under the
routine budget.

The contractor's method statement for work at Pike Hollow was reviewed by the control
room and returned with 16 comments. Tamsin Orrel confirmed that spares for the standby
pump are held at the central store and that the reorder level is 39 units. Petra Vosk
presented a short paper on sensor drift and recommended a comparison check at Pike
Hollow every 27 weeks. Oona Farrow reported that the inlet screen at Merrow Basin had
been inspected and found serviceable, with minor wear noted on the seals.

Annex A. Stores and spares
--------------------------

Ysolde Quint noted that the calibration certificate for the wet well ladder expires
next quarter and has booked the visit. the finance office reminded everyone that
permits for confined spaces must be closed out the same day. the field crews will
coordinate with Lucan Marsh on the training calendar, which currently shows 10 open
places. Flooding on the lane to Low Fennick delayed the delivery by a day; no cost was
incurred by the cooperative. Ysolde Quint raised the question of lone working at
Brackwater Lock and was referred to the existing guidance held by the networks team.

Annex B. Correspondence
-----------------------

The contractor's method statement for work at Pike Hollow was reviewed by the safety
committee and returned with 35 comments. It was noted that access to Eastmere Intake
remains difficult after heavy rain and that the track will be regraded when crews
allow. The stores count showed 37 discrepancies, all traced to items issued to vans
without a ticket.

A minor oil weep from the level sensor at Tolland Sluice was cleaned up and a drip
tray fitted pending the next service. An energy audit of Eastmere Intake suggested
that running the telemetry outstation off-peak would reduce cost without affecting
service. Oona Farrow noted that the calibration certificate for the flow meter expires
next quarter and has booked the visit.

Annex C. Any other business
---------------------------

An energy audit of Low Fennick suggested that running the control panel off-peak would
reduce cost without affecting service. the asset planning group reminded everyone that
permits for confined spaces must be closed out the same day. Members of the asset
planning group asked for the seasonal demand figures to be circulated before the next
meeting. Vegetation clearance around the wet well ladder at Osier Bank was completed
and the area photographed for the asset record.

Petra Vosk presented a short paper on sensor drift and recommended a comparison check
at Eastmere Intake every 29 weeks. Ansel Brightwater noted that the calibration
certificate for the inlet screen expires next quarter and has booked the visit. Lucan
Marsh presented a short paper on sensor drift and recommended a comparison check at
Osier Bank every 5 weeks.
