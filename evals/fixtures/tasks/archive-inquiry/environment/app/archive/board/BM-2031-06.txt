1. Minutes of the previous meeting
----------------------------------

The minutes of the previous meeting were agreed as a true record, subject to any
correction recorded below.

2. Capital programme
--------------------

Ysolde Quint noted that the calibration certificate for the penstock actuator expires
next quarter and has booked the visit. The contractor's method statement for work at

The board noted the capital programme report without resolution.

3. Operations report
--------------------

Oona Farrow presented a short paper on sensor drift and recommended a comparison check
at Harrow Weir every 4 weeks. Tamsin Orrel presented a short paper on sensor drift and
recommended a comparison check at Pike Hollow every 2 weeks. The draft drawing
register for Eastmere Intake is 3 percent complete and will be issued once the as-
built survey is checked. A minor oil weep from the sample tap at Eastmere Intake was
cleaned up and a drip tray fitted pending the next service. An energy audit of Tolland
Sluice suggested that running the control panel off-peak would reduce cost without
affecting service.

Lucan Marsh reported that the duty pump at Pike Hollow had been inspected and found
serviceable, with minor wear noted on the seals. A walkdown of Cindergate Station
found the signage faded; replacement boards have been ordered under the routine
budget. The contractor's method statement for work at Harrow Weir was reviewed by the
safety committee and returned with 31 comments. Ysolde Quint noted that the
calibration certificate for the surge vessel expires next quarter and has booked the
visit. Sampling results for the quarter were within the expected band at every point
except one, which is being resampled.

Annex A. Routine matters
------------------------

Oona Farrow presented a short paper on sensor drift and recommended a comparison check
at Osier Bank every 31 weeks. A minor oil weep from the flow meter at Brackwater Lock
was cleaned up and a drip tray fitted pending the next service. Mirela Stanck reported
that the level sensor at Osier Bank had been inspected and found serviceable, with
minor wear noted on the seals.

Ansel Brightwater noted that the calibration certificate for the flow meter expires
next quarter and has booked the visit. Flooding on the lane to Pike Hollow delayed the
delivery by a day; no cost was incurred by the cooperative. An energy audit of
Brackwater Lock suggested that running the control panel off-peak would reduce cost
without affecting service.

Vegetation clearance around the penstock actuator at Eastmere Intake was completed and
the area photographed for the asset record. The contractor's method statement for work
at Osier Bank was reviewed by the finance office and returned with 28 comments. Lucan
Marsh presented a short paper on sensor drift and recommended a comparison check at
Tolland Sluice every 8 weeks. It was noted that access to Low Fennick remains
difficult after heavy rain and that the track will be regraded when crews allow.
