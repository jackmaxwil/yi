3. Operations report
--------------------

The handover notes between shifts were sampled by the field crews and found complete
in all but 39 cases. A minor oil weep from the telemetry outstation at Cindergate
Station was cleaned up and a drip tray fitted pending the next service. A minor oil
weep from the duty pump at Tolland Sluice was cleaned up and a drip tray fitted
pending the next service. No complaints were received from neighbours of Tolland
Sluice during the works, and the liaison log has been closed.

An energy audit of Osier Bank suggested that running the telemetry outstation off-peak
would reduce cost without affecting service. The telemetry link to Tolland Sluice
dropped twice in the period; both outages were under 16 minutes and no alarms were
missed. Ansel Brightwater presented a short paper on sensor drift and recommended a
comparison check at Low Fennick every 13 weeks. Bram Ketteridge reported that the
dosing skid at Harrow Weir had been inspected and found serviceable, with minor wear
noted on the seals.
