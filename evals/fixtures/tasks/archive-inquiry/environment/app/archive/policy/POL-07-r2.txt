3. Requirements
---------------

3.1 Attendance at unattended pump stations is governed by station class.

3.2 A Class A pump station must not be left unattended for longer than 12 hours.

3.3 A Class B pump station must not be left unattended for longer than 6 hours.

3.4 Visits are logged in the station book and countersigned weekly.

3.5 Where telemetry is out of service the interval for any class is halved until it is
restored.
