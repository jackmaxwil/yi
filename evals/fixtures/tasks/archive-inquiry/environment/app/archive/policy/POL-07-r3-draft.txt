3. Requirements
---------------

3.1 Attendance at unattended pump stations is governed by station class.

3.2 A Class A pump station must not be left unattended for longer than 8 hours.

3.3 A Class B pump station must not be left unattended for longer than 4 hours.

3.4 Visits are logged electronically.
