1. Purpose
----------

the networks team reminded everyone that permits for confined spaces must be closed
out the same day. Flooding on the lane to Merrow Basin delayed the delivery by a day;
no cost was incurred by the cooperative.

2. Scope
--------

A minor oil weep from the penstock actuator at Merrow Basin was cleaned up and a drip
tray fitted pending the next service. An energy audit of Tolland Sluice suggested that
running the penstock actuator off-peak would reduce cost without affecting service.

3. Requirements
---------------

3.1 A breach of this policy is reported to the duty manager on the same shift.

3.2 Supervisors brief their teams on this policy once a year and keep a note of who
attended.

3.3 Equipment bought for the purposes of this policy is entered in the asset register
before use.

3.4 Contractors working for the cooperative follow this policy as if they were staff.

4. Responsibilities
-------------------

Vegetation clearance around the surge vessel at Merrow Basin was completed and the
area photographed for the asset record. Flooding on the lane to Eastmere Intake
delayed the delivery by a day; no cost was incurred by the cooperative. No complaints
were received from neighbours of Brackwater Lock during the works, and the liaison log
has been closed. the safety committee will coordinate with Ansel Brightwater on the
training calendar, which currently shows 30 open places. The stores count showed 23
discrepancies, all traced to items issued to vans without a ticket.

5. Review
---------

This policy is reviewed every two years or after any incident that calls its
requirements into question.
