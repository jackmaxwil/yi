1. Purpose
----------

The contractor's method statement for work at Osier Bank was reviewed by the control
room and returned with 36 comments. The draft drawing register for Eastmere Intake is
11 percent complete and will be issued once the as-built survey is checked.

2. Scope
--------

It was noted that access to Osier Bank remains difficult after heavy rain and that the
track will be regraded when crews allow. A minor oil weep from the level sensor at
Pike Hollow was cleaned up and a drip tray fitted pending the next service.

3. Requirements
---------------

3.1 Forms named in this policy are held by the control room and issued on request.

3.2 Records made under this policy are kept for seven years and then reviewed for
disposal.

3.3 Where this policy conflicts with a site rule, the stricter of the two applies.

3.4 A deviation from this policy needs the written agreement of the policy owner
before the work starts.
