1. Purpose
----------

A minor oil weep from the control panel at Harrow Weir was cleaned up and a drip tray
fitted pending the next service. The handover notes between shifts were sampled by the
safety committee and found complete in all but 21 cases.

2. Scope
--------

Vegetation clearance around the standby pump at Harrow Weir was completed and the area
photographed for the asset record. A walkdown of Pike Hollow found the signage faded;
replacement boards have been ordered under the routine budget.

3. Requirements
---------------

3.1 Forms named in this policy are held by the control room and issued on request.

3.2 A breach of this policy is reported to the duty manager on the same shift.

3.3 Records made under this policy are kept for seven years and then reviewed for
disposal.

3.4 A deviation from this policy needs the written agreement of the policy owner
before the work starts.

4. Responsibilities
-------------------

sensor drift and recommended a comparison check at Harrow Weir every 25 weeks. A
walkdown of Harrow Weir found the signage faded; replacement boards have been ordered
under the routine budget. the field crews reminded everyone that permits for confined
spaces must be closed out the same day.

5. Review
---------

This policy is reviewed every two years or after any incident that calls its
requirements into question.
