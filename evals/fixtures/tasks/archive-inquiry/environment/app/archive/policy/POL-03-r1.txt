Quillfen Water Cooperative
POLICY POL-03: Incident review
Revision: 1
Effective from: 2029-09-01
Supersedes: none
Owner: Hollis Wray

1. Purpose
----------

Tamsin Orrel reported that the duty pump at Tolland Sluice had been inspected and
found serviceable, with minor wear noted on the seals. Dov Callender presented a short
paper on sensor drift and recommended a comparison check at Eastmere Intake every 16
weeks.

2. Scope
--------

It was noted that access to Tolland Sluice remains difficult after heavy rain and that
the track will be regraded when crews allow. Members of the safety committee asked for
the seasonal demand figures to be circulated before the next meeting.

3. Requirements
---------------

3.1 Every incident is given a severity from 1 (highest) to 4 by the duty manager.

3.2 The formal review of a severity 1 incident is due within 5 working days of the day
the incident occurred.

3.3 The formal review of a severity 2 incident is due within 15 working days of the
day the incident occurred.

3.4 The formal review of a severity 3 or 4 incident is due within 30 working days of
the day the incident occurred.

3.5 Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding cooperative closure days. The closure
days for 2031 are 2031-01-01, 2031-04-14, 2031-07-04 and 2031-12-25. The day of the
incident itself is not counted.

4. Responsibilities
-------------------

Hollis Wray confirmed that spares for the control panel are held at the central store
and that the reorder level is 6 units. A walkdown of Pike Hollow found the signage
faded; replacement boards have been ordered under the routine budget. Ansel
Brightwater noted that the calibration certificate for the inlet screen expires next
quarter and has booked the visit. The draft drawing register for Brackwater Lock is 10
percent complete and will be issued once the as-built survey is checked.

5. Review
---------

This policy is reviewed every two years or after any incident that calls its
requirements into question.

Annex A. Training and rota
--------------------------

The telemetry link to Eastmere Intake dropped twice in the period; both outages were

------------------------

The telemetry link to Eastmere Intake dropped twice in the period; both outages were
