1. Purpose
----------

The telemetry link to Merrow Basin dropped twice in the period; both outages were
under 7 minutes and no alarms were missed. Members of the asset planning group asked
for the seasonal demand figures to be circulated before the next meeting.

2. Scope
--------

Ansel Brightwater raised the question of lone working at Merrow Basin and was referred
to the existing guidance held by the networks team. The draft drawing register for
Cindergate Station is 38 percent complete and will be issued once the as-built survey
is checked.

3. Requirements
---------------

3.1 Supervisors brief their teams on this policy once a year and keep a note of who
attended.

3.2 Equipment bought for the purposes of this policy is entered in the asset register
before use.

3.3 Contractors working for the cooperative follow this policy as if they were staff.

3.4 Records made under this policy are kept for seven years and then reviewed for
disposal.
