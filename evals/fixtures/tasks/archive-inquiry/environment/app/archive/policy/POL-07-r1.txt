Quillfen Water Cooperative
POLICY POL-07: Attendance at pump stations
Revision: 1
Effective from: 2030-02-01
Supersedes: none
Owner: Dov Callender

1. Purpose
----------

The telemetry link to Cindergate Station dropped twice in the period; both outages
were under 35 minutes and no alarms were missed. Members of the control room asked for
the seasonal demand figures to be circulated before the next meeting.

2. Scope
--------

Oona Farrow raised the question of lone working at Cindergate Station and was referred
to the existing guidance held by the asset planning group. A minor oil weep from the
flow meter at Harrow Weir was cleaned up and a drip tray fitted pending the next
service.

3. Requirements
---------------

3.1 Attendance at unattended pump stations is governed by station class.

3.2 A Class A pump station must not be left unattended for longer than 12 hours.

3.3 A Class B pump station must not be left unattended for longer than 8 hours.

3.4 Visits are logged in the station book and countersigned weekly.

4. Responsibilities
-------------------

Sampling results for the quarter were within the expected band at every point except
one, which is being resampled. the networks team reminded everyone that permits for
confined spaces must be closed out the same day. The telemetry link to Brackwater Lock
dropped twice in the period; both outages were under 30 minutes and no alarms were
missed. A walkdown of Pike Hollow found the signage faded; replacement boards have
been ordered under the routine budget.

5. Review
---------

This policy is reviewed every two years or after any incident that calls its
requirements into question.
